# Working method and release requirements

The user's editing method is binding for future screenplay work. See the detailed [screenplay instructions](../screenplay/AGENTS.md), which remain intact.

## Drafting unit

Work on a small connected interaction, usually a handful of turns plus necessary action. Read the full scene and relevant downstream material. Do not generate a wholesale replacement screenplay or treat a fifty-page opening as one drafting unit. Internal micro-edits do not each require the user's approval.

Before editing, identify what each participant knows, believes, wants and fears; what changes during the encounter; and what later scenes depend on. A stronger line cannot invent harm, guilt, access, powers or knowledge. A language pass holds the story fixed unless a separate change is authorized.

Save coherent changes to Git with stable filenames. Do not keep adding revision-numbered directories. Do not resume the abandoned chunk/import workflow or fabricate old commit timestamps. Current work belongs on the agreed branch; changing main, deleting other branches or rewriting history is a separate decision.

## Review sequence

Diagnose the exact local problem; explore alternatives; select the revision; reread into and out of it; verify dependencies. Then read whole conversations both without and with their action. Finally make a separate connected-scene regression pass. Pages provide coverage; interaction boundaries need not obey page breaks.

## Blocking gates

| Gate | Failure that reopens it |
|---|---|
| Responsive dialogue | Alternating audience-information speeches; reply to an unstated question; unmotivated change of subject |
| Variable rhythm | Routine long briefings, serial clipped declarations, mechanical ellipses or compulsory deflection |
| Breathing room | Gestures and microblocking fill every gap; quiet is explained instead of experienced |
| Clear phrasing | Undefined objects decide outcomes, forced quips, recaps of action just shown, unsupported referents |
| Distinct voice | Everyone speaks as the same therapist, officer or exposition device; Tessa inherits Neri's temperament |
| Emotional consequence | Denial restores trust, isolated outburst changes nothing, grief exists only in a prop |
| Physical continuity | Closed/open door contradictions, occupied hands, teleported objects, unexplained injury or geography |
| Knowledge and powers | Audience truth leaks to characters, expertise vanishes, a demonstrated option is forgotten |
| Earned outcomes | Convenient documents or witnesses, permission without stakes, enemies losing competence to serve the plot |
| Protected story | Unannounced changes to chronology, identity, capability, promises, trust or endpoint |
| Attention and duration | Procedural proof crowds out people; shorter length is treated as proof of better writing |
| Coverage/integration | Unreviewed material, orphaned replies or stale checks after another edit |
| Export integrity | PDF differs from reviewed source, clipping or stranded cues/headings, later edits invalidate proof |

Each decision needs an actual passage, the challenge tested, the reason for disposition, the connected recheck and the exact candidate. Revised is not synonymous with passed. Existing author-side judgment remains open to new reader findings.

An unreviewed applicable gate or unresolved source defect blocks final PDF delivery. A pending actor read does not excuse a plainly identifiable source problem. Word counts, number of passes and clean rendering do not certify naturalness or emotional credibility.

Independent cold reads, actor/table reads and timed performance have not been performed for Rebuild 11. Do not invent them. Source checks and layout verification are different work.
