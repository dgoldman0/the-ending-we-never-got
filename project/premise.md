# Premise, narrative levels and tone

Status: protected direction unless a paragraph explicitly says draft or open. Recovered from the user's decisions in the development conversation; the earlier integrated screenplay supplies additional draft examples.

Current production focus (16 September 2026): adapt Tessa's original timeline into a Ren'Py visual novel. The larger narrative and its incursion continuation below remain preserved for later work. Their framing and original-opening duration do not set the scope or playtime of the current VN. See the [adaptation brief](../visual-novel/README.md).

## The story being told

Neri adored a fictional heroine while living as an ordinary human in roughly the present-day/near-future era. She then survived into an ageless civilization, lived for millions of years, and became an Astravus with an immense company of bonded nonhuman companions. After reincarnation into a young High Human body in another world, she eventually recognizes that world as the setting of the visual novel she loved when she was young.

That heroine is Tessa: an ordinary nineteen-year-old summoned without consent as a Saint. Tessa's original story is an earnest, painful heroic life. She gains competence and meaningful attachments, centrally brings about the Demon King's downfall, and survives at severe personal cost. Neri's presence transforms the course of that life, preserving people and possibilities the original story destroyed.

Neri is brilliant, cunning, playful, whimsical and capable of explosive affection. Her ancient age does not make her an emotionally remote sage. Her delight in meeting Tessa, watching her succeed and sharing her life is a central source of comedy and tenderness.

## Narrative levels

| Level | What exists there | Important distinction |
|---|---|---|
| The fans' original reality | A visual novel and later anime about Tessa; ordinary human fans; subsequent longevity and Astravus development | Neri lives into the far future. The fans who later become Iven and Mara die young, before agelessness. |
| The source VN's original route | Tessa's summoning, war, relationships, losses, victory and bittersweet continuation | Native Iven and Mara have no Earth memories or knowledge of the VN. |
| The altered world | Neri, and reincarnated versions of Iven and Mara, were born and grew up here before Tessa's summoning | Their memories coexist with genuine local families, professions and histories. |
| Tessa's Earth | The life and mother from whom Tessa was taken | Its exact ontological relationship to the fans' Earth remains open. Do not silently collapse the two. |
| The audience's experience | Original tragedy first, followed by Neri's birth and the transformed story | Present the original as immediate drama rather than announcing a recap or treating its suffering as a joke. |

## Opening structure

The first substantial section tells Tessa's unaltered route in accelerated but emotionally developed form. It ends at postwar Bellweir. The work then returns to Neri's birth years before the summoning and follows her local childhood into recognition of Tessa's world around the time of the summoning.

The approximate intention was thirty minutes for the original telling, not a hard limit. The current original screenplay has grown beyond that: Rebuild 11's delivered copy has 57 screenplay pages plus a title page. Runtime has not been measured. Preserve this as an allocation issue, not permission to pad or abruptly compress the story.

## Media premise

The source work is a visual novel, later adapted into anime. Interactive attachment is important: readers/players have spent time choosing Tessa's replies, revisiting scenes and trying to obtain a better outcome. In the earlier draft, saved games repeatedly return to the bridge, but the canonical successful route cannot save everyone.

The draft title is **A Light for the Last Winter**. It appears in the integrated screenplay but was invented during drafting; it remains a working title. Publication dates, route architecture, editions and adaptation changes are not finalized. Do not manufacture an exact release year from incidental dialogue.

The deeper history of humanity initiating the war can be explicit and reliable optional VN lore, available to players who read the background material. It need not be disputed, vague or unknown to Iven and Mara. Tessa's original on-screen route can leave her unaware of information available to players.

## Two distinct emotional registers

Original Tessa is earnest and endearing under intense pressure. Her appeal comes from particular preferences, affection, courage, stubbornness, developing skill and a life worth caring about. Her story contains enjoyment, but its overall course is brutally painful and bittersweet. Do not make her behave like a whimsical OP heroine throughout that route.

The altered story allows the affection, cuteness, exuberance and over-the-top effects associated with the reference set. Neri's power changes consequences. It need not preserve deaths, mutilation or sacrifice so Tessa can 'earn' a heroic identity. Tessa still acts, learns, chooses relationships, disagrees and helps people.

## Endpoint

Valcair's war schemes cease to produce their intended effects. His expectations are repeatedly overwhelmed; circumstances and relationships change until his interest in war falls apart. Peace becomes something he wants. The climax must not revert to Neri conventionally defeating a stronger enemy or humiliating a conquered king into compliance.

A positive Tessa–Demon Prince romance is an intended possibility; the earlier full-feature draft realizes it. The exact romantic structure, pacing and ending still need deliberate confirmation. Neri's intense affection is protected; the exact category of that bond is not settled merely by affectionate staging.
