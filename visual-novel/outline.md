## The experience to build

Follow Tessa from an interrupted grocery errand into a life she never consented to, then let the player spend enough time with the people she comes to love. Her growing competence must remain visible alongside the narrowing possibilities: the promise of home fails, Bellweir falls, cooperation briefly becomes possible, Harrow destroys that promise, and victory leaves permanent losses. The final Bellweir scene gives her a continuing life and an unresolved search for home. Only after that ending does the game ask the player whether they wish to save her.

**Working structure: one original route, with optional investigation and room to linger.** All players see the essential relationships, confrontations and consequences. Investigation changes what the player understands, rather than silently creating a rescue route or giving the cast information they lack. A branch structure that changes events would be a separate adaptation decision.

Proposed reading flow:

`Rovel → Bellweir → The broken promise → Gray Scar → Across the river → Harrow → After Harrow → The witness → The citadel → Bellweir again → final question`

The player can Look closer at meaningful details, follow connections into additional authored material, and revisit them through Threads. The five quiet boundaries below remain candidate availability points; avoid turning them into obligatory interruptions. The historical thread establishes that humans began the ancient conflict through the first Saint summoning. There is no clue quota, comprehension quiz, relationship score, countdown or requirement to replay the tragedy to reach the question.

Make room for this through the screenplay's existing time jumps and compact illustrated narration for repeated visits and campaigning. Preserve the interactions that establish attachment and change relationships. The [pacing plan](pacing.md) identifies the specific stretches to compress and the causes each transition must retain.

## Viewpoint, dialogue and interaction

- Keep Tessa as the emotional center. Use the screenplay's dialogue and actions as the first draft of each interaction; convert only the action that cannot be conveyed visually into concise present-tense narration. An elaborate new internal monologue is not assumed.
- Retain the screenplay's cutaways in their existing order: Elin's inquiry, Valcair/Vask's decisions, Lucan's work and the northern family's loss. Use clear location/time changes. These scenes are part of the route, not clues the player can accidentally miss.
- The player advances dialogue, observes action and can examine selected objects or compare information. Most scene direction remains authored. Quiet pauses can be held or advanced; essential story meaning never depends on noticing a tiny hotspot.
- An inspection belongs to the player unless the scene expressly gives the object or information to a character. The interface's collected material is not Tessa's bag or Elin's notebook.
- If conversation options are later added, begin with topic order or optional follow-up, then rejoin the unchanged source interaction. Do not offer an apparent choice to refuse the whole deployment, forgive Lucan, rescue Iven or leave Mara when every option would secretly force the same result. No new dialogue options are written in this pass.
- The final question is a deliberate change in whom the game addresses. Before it, avoid a rescue meter, ominous locked route labels or a tutorial that promises a better ending through correct play.

## Candidate chapters

### 01 — Rovel

**Source:** S001–S005; arrival through the infirmary window-seat conversation. Time: nineteen, the first days after summoning.

Open at the interrupted stride, with the ordinary message from her mother, the dropped groceries and the briefly visible store. Present arrival, attempted return and the closed arch as successive beats. Let the apartment establish confinement through the locked door, dead phone and drawing, rather than introductory lore. Olan's treatment establishes both the usefulness and limits of Tessa's light. The public ceremony turns that success into an obligation she did not accept. Iven promises to accompany her; end with Tessa making room on the sill and Iven sitting beside her.

**VN treatment:** the summoning study anchors tone, not a finished runtime environment. Source-specific arrival/closure states and compact Tessa/Senn exchanges need coherent direction. The apartment combines a compact exchange, blocked-exit action and a quiet drawing view. Olan's treatment needs clear multi-person attribution and before/after states retaining his missing fingers. The ceremony needs the complete formal outfit and the full acting group, followed by a quieter two-person frame at the window.

**Player space:** optional examination of source-grounded details and meaningful relationships, with exact return and revisiting through Threads. R01 labels this candidate span, not a mandatory chapter-end screen. Define the payoff before producing a connection. None can restore the phone or reopen the arch.

**What changes:** Tessa's demand to get home now exists alongside a particular person's need for her and Iven's limited, practical support.

### 02 — Bellweir

**Source:** S006–S009; convoy training, the inn, market and boat outing. Time: five weeks on the road, then the first summer.

Preserve the training's sequence of mistakes and corrections, Iven's burned dinner, the checker game and Mara giving Tessa control of her room key. Introduce Bellweir through the turning heron, drawing, Hest's family and shared work. Give the entire boat interaction space: teasing, Iven's portrait, the mention of her mother and learning to row with Mara.

**VN treatment:** short visual demonstrations during training, then settled conversational framing. Return to the drawing motif without turning every sketch into a collectible challenge. The intact market and fountain must be memorable enough to recognize when damaged and rebuilt. The new early working outfit replaces the superseded convoy costume.

**Player space:** optional lingering on the finished interaction, drawings or the heron's mechanism. A playable checker or rowing minigame is unnecessary for this first proposal. The key, meal, humor and relationships remain mandatory.

**What changes:** this becomes a place and company Tessa can care about while still expecting to go home.

### 03 — The broken promise

**Source:** S010–S017; the attack decision, Bellweir's destruction, Elin's inquiry, displacement and the hearing. Time: eight months after summoning, then the first winter.

Keep Valcair's refusal of the proposed agreement before the attack. During the evacuation, show why extending sanctuary toward Orren exposes the people already beneath it. Move through Elin's risky access and the scholar's admission in their source order, then the displaced family and Tessa learning what the book actually contains. Play the hearing and the corridor exchange fully: she gains specific freedoms, Elin loses archive access, Senn keeps the original, and Mara's earlier coercion remains unresolved.

**VN treatment:** reuse the market's recognizable geography in its destruction state. Cut from the scale of the collapse to the boots, cap and revised search portrait. The hearing needs careful speaker/door staging rather than a sequence of floating talking heads. Tessa leaves the mantle; the temple badge remains available for Gray Scar.

**Player space:** R02 opens after the corridor scene. Compare what was promised, what was missing and what was conceded. No playable rescue allocation at Bellweir and no clue needed to make Tessa confront Senn.

**What changes:** going home is an unresolved problem, not a promised reward for service. Her continuing work becomes a decision she has more control over.

### 04 — Gray Scar

**Source:** S018–S025; the shared rescue, picket, Lucan's loss of command, paid search and Mara's sanction. Time: second summer, then the following days.

Introduce Lucan in action with threatened civilians, not through a romance introduction. Keep the mistrust inside the ferryhouse, the negotiated weapons/boat terms, and his voluntary return after his own families are safe. Serat needs a surgeon despite Tessa's help. Mara keeps her word at the picket, Tessa gives Lucan Orren's portrait, and both Mara and Lucan face consequences afterward.

**VN treatment:** establish the ferryhouse, exposed bank, gate, rock shelter and two boats before rapid cuts. Reuse the training's readable sanctuary behavior. Let Lucan's return for the litter be a held decision beat. His departure from his father's room and paid boat crew belong to his own cutaway, with no Tessa commentary.

**Player space:** quiet inspection of the rescue geography or terms can be revisited after the emergency. Keep the emergency itself continuous. No action test determines whether Lucan goes back or Serat receives care.

**What changes:** specific people become trustworthy through costly actions without removing the conflict between their institutions.

### 05 — Across the river

**Source:** S026–S031; finding Orren, camp access, reunions, repeated visits, the laughing portrait and exchange negotiations. Time: six weeks after Gray Scar through autumn.

The apprentice's indirect message gives Lucan a lead; his status alone does not produce access. Preserve the dispute over lending the boat to the quarry and the recovery conditions. Give Hest and Orren's reunion its own sustained interaction. The search for the second apprentice, Lucan's account of the enormous cap and Tessa's drawing build attachment alongside the work. End with the exchange order and Lucan offering his launch for the apprentice's release.

**VN treatment:** the camp infirmary can recur with changing dressings, personal belongings and conversational groupings. Keep the successive-visits montage compact: a few views and narration carry the cap, improved leg support, returned launch, quarry refusal and barge sighting. Keep the subsequent laughing-portrait interaction in full. Do not add an unwritten romantic confession or make treatment a dating reward.

**Player space:** R03 after the exchange agreement. The player may compare access conditions, the two caps, the search portrait and the boat's changing use. All reunions and affection-building interactions stay in the main route.

**What changes:** practical cooperation grows into affection and a credible expectation that families can come home. Harrow will break a promise the player has watched people work to make possible.

### 06 — Harrow

**Source:** S032–S037; inspection and promise, planning, cargo tampering, collapse, confrontation and grief.

Keep the inspection before Valcair's order, and the cargo-shed cutaway before the crossing. The player knows the catastrophe is being prepared. Tessa does not. Preserve her reliance on Lucan's promise to remain beside Orren's cart and the source's explanation of where he actually is when the clock strikes. Show the complete rescue sequence: Renn freed, family moved to stone, Iven nearly safe, brace/planking failure, torn sleeve and failed light beneath him. Follow with Tessa's confrontation and the coat scene. Hest feeding the northern searcher remains the final human action of the chapter.

**VN treatment:** separate the intact crossing, midspan collapse and southern remnant; readable geography carries the action. Hold the sleeve/coat afterward without an explanatory recap of the battle. Avoid using a barrage of effects to replace the physical causes.

**Player space:** no timed input, apparent save-Iven choice, result grade or immediate evidence-board interruption. Let the player pause/save normally. Investigation resumes in the next chapter after the aftermath has unfolded.

**What changes:** Iven is dead; Tessa's trust and personal access to Lucan are broken. The player's knowledge of his actions does not repair that relationship.

### 07 — After Harrow

**Source:** S038–S043; Lucan's blocked inquiry, the recruited son, the mill-town fight and changing seasons. Time: two weeks, later weeks, three months after Harrow, then subsequent seasons and the following year.

Let the false public account and northern son's recruitment show the atrocity's further consequences. In the mill town, preserve the failed direct attempts before Tessa and Olan reach the wardkeeper. The cart aftermath stays attached to the killing: her inability to sheath the sword, wanting Iven and eventually taking Mara's hand matter as much as the victory. Keep the seasonal montage as three short illustrated narrative blocks—spring, the following summer and winter—intercut with Lucan's continuing failure to reach Marren. Do not expand them into separate campaign missions.

**VN treatment:** change clothing, physical wear, patients and occupied places across time. The empty summer village and unfinished drawing need a quiet beat. Do not imply that Tessa has been in constant dirt, that her final hand injury has already occurred or that Mara is already absent.

**Player space:** R04 after the seasonal sequence. Reconstruct Harrow from the information the player has actually seen, distinguish it from public propaganda, and continue the optional historical inquiry. The latter's new evidence still needs authoring.

**What changes:** the war has become years of lived strain. Effective action has not relieved the grief, and obtaining a witness has become dangerous work in its own right.

### 08 — The witness

**Source:** S044–S049; extraction, Marren's refuge, father/son confrontation, infirmary defense, testimony and preparations. Time: late campaign, nearly four years after summoning.

Preserve the entire causal chain: blocked access leads to extraction, extraction exposes the infirmary, Lucan refuses his father's demand, some captains leave, others defend, and Marren reaches Mara under protection. His testimony establishes his signature and limited observations. Tessa's questions and the separate letter remain in their existing order. Olan checks the approach independently; Lucan's plan is useful but old. End on preparations and Iven's medical bag being handed to the healer.

**VN treatment:** give Lucan's physical extraction a clear route and injury continuity. Contrast his father's dry coat with the threatened ward, without inventing a sentimental flashback. Keep clearance, statement, personal letter and the two plans visually distinct. The contents of the personal letter are not supplied by the screenplay: do not manufacture them for an inspectable prop.

**Player space:** R05 at the chapter boundary, before the stair sequence begins. Compare testimony with what the player witnessed and review the plans' limits. This is the last proposed opening for completing the player-only historical investigation before the climax.

**What changes:** enough evidence and cooperation exist to act; personal forgiveness, certainty and safety do not follow automatically.

### 09 — The citadel

**Source:** S050–S057; the goodbye, parallel defenses, confrontation, Mara's death, hand examination and limited stand-down.

Start with Tessa fastening Mara's trembling guard and their promise to meet afterward. Preserve the cuts between the two military fronts. At the entrance ward, Tessa commits to the shrinking opening while Mara holds the stair. The throne-hall fight demonstrates changing tactics against renewed protection; Tessa suffers the right-hand wound, takes the sword in her left, defeats Valcair and opens the entrance. Follow the source into finding Mara, the surgeon's uncertainty and the lower gate's guarded medical agreement.

**VN treatment:** give combat a small number of legible states and clear hand/weapon changes. The entrance release and Valcair's personal protection are separate. The storm-infirmary study anchors the examination's dreariness. No victory celebration between the fight and finding Mara; no romance reconciliation when Lucan is brought to the stair.

**Player space:** authored action and uninterrupted aftermath. No combat scoring, replay hint or hidden fast-input solution to saving Mara or Tessa's hand. The seal attribution in the confrontation remains a flagged source question, not silently rewritten dialogue.

**What changes:** Tessa wins the military confrontation. Mara is dead, her drawing hand is permanently affected, and the ceasefire leaves accountability unresolved.

### 10 — Bellweir again

**Source:** S058, then the user-requested VN question. Time: spring, four years after summoning; Tessa about twenty-three.

Keep the rebuilt town's remaining damage, the heron's missing wing and inspectors from both banks. Tessa chooses Bellweir over temple accommodation, writes with her left hand, receives an uncertain book lead and looks at her mother's kitchen and Iven's portrait. Ada calls; Renn carries plates; Orren makes room; Tessa takes the chair. Preserve that final action and the fade to black.

**VN treatment:** return to familiar market/bakery framing, with visibly older children and Tessa's accumulated wear. This is ordinary spring light with the established discomfort, not a visual cure or a world with no warmth left. The new postwar outfit remains to be designed. Do not overlay the question on her grief-stricken face or introduce a voice addressing her.

After a separate, reader-paced transition on black, display exactly:

> Do you wish to save Tessa?
