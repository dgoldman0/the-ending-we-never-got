## Cross-review

The lead reviewed every surviving full figure and bust, the banner back, and native details of faces, hands, feet, badges and cloth repairs. Separate agents compared identity, ages and wear across the designs, and independently checked practical clothing construction, left-side badges, sword suspension, gloves and boots. The selected storm-infirmary face and accepted working portraits remained the identity references; rejected clothing keys were not used as image inputs.

The review changed the files: both ceremonial busts gained headroom; inaccurate sun-ray counts were repaired locally; a misplaced field sleeve patch was removed; pale marks from the banner sun repair were healed and rechecked. Final bust top margins cluster around 100–112 pixels on 1254-square alpha canvases. Full figures are 1024×1536. Makers reopened the costume XCFs and verified their visible exports against the PNGs.

The surviving ceremony designs use thin draping fabric, unpadded shoulders and restrained gold work. The banner alternative is most distinctive from behind; its bust shows only a narrow lapis edge. The practical outfits differ through collar, trim and silhouette while remaining in human-side blues. Campaign Tessa shows deeper lower-eye shadows, drawn cheeks and etched fatigue beyond the younger field state. Its badge is cleaner than the worn surrounding coat, a minor finish difference retained in this proposal.
