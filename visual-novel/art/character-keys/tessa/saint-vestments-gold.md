The waist is gathered by a narrow woven cord. There is no visible undershirt, separate collar, corset or open coat.

A separately removable, lightweight white ceremonial mantle lies close to the shoulders and falls in long fine folds. A small brass twelve-ray sun joins its front edges across the upper chest. Fine gold-thread rays spread from that clasp across the mantle, with botanical stitchwork and plentiful white space. The Saint alone wears pure white, whereas the priests wear cream; the gold rays throughout the cloth distinguish this option without rigid ornament around her face. The robe carries a small twelve-ray sun near its lower front. Pale slippers complete the outfit.
