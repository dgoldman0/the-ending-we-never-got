## Construction and period

S058, the final Bellweir scene, four years after summoning: Tessa is about twenty-three. Her right hand rests in a soft brace; she writes with her left. Iven and Mara have died, and remaining in Bellweir does not resolve her grief or route home. The standing pose is a costume reference, not a new screenplay event.

One soft dusty-mulberry wool-linen wrap robe reaches the ankles. Its right front crosses to the anatomical left ribcage and waist, secured by two wooden toggles in large cloth loops reachable by her healthy left hand. The low, modest diagonal neckline lies flat: both visible edges are the same robe overlapping itself. Nothing is visible underneath. Long sleeves, restrained tonal botanical woven edging and low oxblood slip-on shoes retain the adopted world's material language without a Saint uniform, modern cardigan or extra shawl. No belt, separate yoke or badge is proposed.

The pale soft brace covers the anatomical right wrist and hand, leaving fingers exposed. She protects it near her abdomen; the bare left hand hangs separately. The full figure shows the closures and brace together. The bust deliberately excludes hands and demonstrates the continuous chest, diagonal neckline and upper toggle.

## Review and limits

Inspected the complete GIMP export, native-resolution face, both hands, feet and cloth closures, then compared the full-figure face and bust directly with the selected storm-infirmary likeness. The oval face, substantial straight nose, broad mouth, ordinary brown eyes and untidy chestnut hair remain recognizable. The initial full figure was too softly youthful; a targeted condition revision reduced facial fullness and strengthened sleep loss and weathering. The selected figure and companion bust have drawn cheeks, uneven freckled skin, tired lower eyelids, prematurely etched tension and guarded attention. They do not smile, model confidently or rely on a cosmetic pout. Whether this fully captures the ending's emotional weight remains for the user's judgment.

At bust size the garment reads as one wrap robe, with one mulberry field and a diagonal opening. The outline and neckline differ from the working ivory yoke, closed ceremonial collar, winter hood and campaign standing collar. The full figure has a relaxed continuous skirt and ordinary body proportions; the brace stays on her right and the toggles on her left. No clothes stack, visible undershirt, modern cardigan or added mantle appears.

The bust was separately painted from this proposal, not cropped from the small full-figure face or mirrored. GIMP reduced its content uniformly from 1254 to 1180 pixels and placed it on a 1254-square alpha canvas (37 pixels horizontal offset, 74 vertical) to give the hair clear headroom. No body region was stretched. The image remains a costume study, not a runtime expression or an in-game visual approval.
