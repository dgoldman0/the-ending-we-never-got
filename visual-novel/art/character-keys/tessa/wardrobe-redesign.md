## What failed

The previous designs began with a modern-looking shirt, trousers, jacket and boots, then signaled Saint status with a pin, mantle or expensive trim. The formal variant changed every garment but still resembled tailored contemporary formalwear. The postwar cardigan repeated the same underlying visual vocabulary. Reusing those images carried the problem into the convoy study.

The missing work is a coherent clothing tradition for this world's Saint: recognizable shapes, textile construction, materials, ornament, and how the person wearing them changes them. Formal, everyday and campaign clothes need to belong to that tradition while remaining distinct actual outfits.

It does not show the source-required inner pocket, prove a long stride or validate rowing, kneeling, a full sword draw and casting. Resolve those specific mechanics when adapting the relevant action.
