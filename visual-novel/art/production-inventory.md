## Review and delivery status

A key becomes **reviewed working visual** only after full-frame and native-crop inspection. Record any GIMP work and verify delivered XCF/PNG pairs. User selection remains a separate status. The inventory does not authorize an image to contradict the screenplay, and a queued scene does not authorize an added narrative beat.
