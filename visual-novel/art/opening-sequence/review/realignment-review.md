## Lighting and editable work

The Intense exports replace the earlier comfortable grades from the corrected GIMP masters. They do not accumulate another grade on already clipped exports. Bright temple light now reaches faces, hands and surrounding stone together. Earth's fluorescent store remains separately masked. Ordinary ward daylight is colder and more abrasive; night leaves faces and most room detail in deep shadow. Text has its own dark surface and subdued night color.

GIMP inspection exposed a real masking error: Script-Fu treats numeric zero as true, while GIMP's `FALSE` is zero. An explicit boolean comparison now prevents the Earth mask from being applied to unrelated scenes and sprites. The milk and phone shots have their own portal boundaries. The chair-barricade shot received a narrowly masked reflection on the chair and hand from the underlying source; lifting its crushed-black export caused banding and was discarded. The face and room remain dark. The final native barricade capture shows the grip, chair back and floor contact without a lit portrait.

Original masters remain in `../masters/`; replacement grades and retained repairs are in `../relight-masters/`. The arrival, Tessa phone sprite, chair barricade and standing-ward masters were reopened and independently exported; their visible pixels matched runtime exports. This checks reproducibility, not artistic quality.

The user's later request adds **Settings → Lighting → Intense / Softened**, with Intense as the default. Softened restores the earlier exposure of these same opening drawings, including sprites and discovery crops. Title art and legacy fallbacks also honor the preference. The [lighting record](../../lighting/README.md) distinguishes restored exports from new GIMP alternates; both treatments must receive future continuity fixes.

## Interface observations and corrections

The new aged-gilt/mineral frame was generated as interface material and separated in GIMP. Narration uses a quieter horizontal edge; speech has an asymmetric botanical frame beside the speaking figure. Menus and the title use related surfaces and a cropped crest. The editable frame and crest masters were reopened and their exports compared with runtime assets.

Native inspection of long and larger-text dialogue exposed leaf/text collisions. Speech padding and line widths were corrected. Small selected buttons initially compressed the ornament into jagged marks; these now use a quiet tint and underlined text. The current settings screen fits at 1280×720 without clipped help text. Names, gestures and expressions remain unobstructed in the inspected promise, doorway, priest, Olan and mother shots. This is evidence of layout correction, not a claim that the interface meets the user's artistic bar.
