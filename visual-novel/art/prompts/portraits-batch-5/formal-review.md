# Batch 5 formal Tessa source portraits

Six separate built-in imagegen paintings for Part C, after the user selected A: `tessa-{attentive,hurt,resolute}-formal-{speaking,listening}.png` in `renpy/game/art/portraits/`. Exact prompts are the six matching text files in this directory. This is source-art review, not game crop, lighting or connected-scene clearance.

Read S004–S005 in `screenplay/original-timeline/source.fountain` before painting. Tessa is nineteen, healthy and unwilling to be displayed as the Saint; the ceremonial costume remains present for both parts of the exchange. The selected storm-infirmary face anchors identity, while `saint-vestments-gold-bust.png` anchors only the accepted light white robe, shallow round neckline, thin removable white mantle and fine gold botanical rays from the chest clasp. The first speaking generation also used the accepted natural working-speaking face, explicitly excluding its clothing.

All six were inspected as full compositions, then native face/hair/clasp crops and 150-pixel light/dark composites. Their eyes, nose, broad mouth, low brown ponytail, freckles and uneven skin remain the same person. Speaking poses look right with open speech shapes; listeners look left with closed mouths. The attentive speaker is concerned and searching rather than smiling; the attentive listener has the least brow tension. Hurt speaking raises the inner brows and makes the question vulnerable, while hurt listening drops the gaze and draws in the mouth. Resolute speaking advances the chin and sets the jaw; resolute listening holds a direct, harder gaze. These are restrained acting differences rather than caricature. A few minute decorative stitch shapes vary; the garment construction, shallow neck, delicate mantle drape and gold botanical language stay fixed.

The generated headroom varied. GIMP uniformly scaled each original 1254-square source to 1166 square and placed it at (44, 88) in the original 1254-square canvas, preserving proportions and a natural bottom edge. GIMP alpha-level normalization corrected resampling's 254 maximum to 255. Final files retain real RGBA transparency, with visible-hair top margins about 101–160 pixels. Fine hair gaps were checked on both light and dark grounds; preview color artifacts do not appear in the composites. Counted twelve short rays at each brass clasp. No hands, duplicated collars, heavy ceremonial layers or rigid fins are present.

The runtime crop and grade scripts were intentionally not run; Claude's in-game checks remain the next review stage. No rejected costume image was supplied as a generation reference.
