## Shared geography proposal

The screenplay fixes two stone approaches over a deep, fast channel, a covered timber middle span, north tollhouse/clock and cargo shed, and a distinct south stone pier with an iron brace. It does not specify metric dimensions or camera bearings. A workable production design is a roughly 25–30 m crossing with a 4–4.5 m timber deck: one cart lane and enough pedestrian clearance for the opposite-moving family. These dimensions are provisional and should be adjusted together if the first composition needs it.

Choose the main exterior camera on the downstream side, looking upriver, with **north on screen left and south on screen right**. For this proposal the river flows toward this camera. Keep that screen direction for collapse/rescue/bank views; an on-deck reverse angle needs visible landmarks and an explicit note, not a mirrored bridge.

- **North, screen left:** square timber clock case above the tollhouse, pale dial/dark hands, one bell in the open upper frame. A broad approach holds waiting medical carts and the two return-tool wagons in single file. A lockable cargo shed stands back from the northern road, off the crossing lane, with wide wagon doors and a separate personnel door. The courier arrives on that road and calls Lucan back at the north entrance. This is where Lucan remains when the span falls.
- **Middle:** heavy timber deck and covered roof/truss, open side bays, a continuous cart path and separate plain parapet. The two short low tool wagons can occupy the central span one behind the other, physically distant from Orren's cart at the south end. Small empty charge housings are fixed inspection fittings on the bridge structure, identifiable as separate objects from cargo under wagon floors. Do not build obvious live explosives into the inspected housings.
- **South, screen right:** a substantial intact stone pier/abutment and landward approach. A short final timber bay projects from it over the channel, with an identifiable diagonal bent iron brace beneath that bay and a bolted anchor in the stone. The brace can catch the buckled remnant, then tear free while the stone remains sound. The structure must leave a plausible arm's reach from kneeling Tessa on the last boards to Mara braced directly behind her on stone.
- **Downstream south bank:** a footpath descends beside the stone pier to a muddy search-boat landing and open receiving ground. It is below and downstream of the bridge, not another intact river crossing. Farther downriver is separate space for the northern widow, son and pear sapling. The relief camp sits back on accessible southern ground with a tent opening toward that search/receiving area.

The north clock exists before the catastrophe, but S032 occurs **the previous day** (Lucan says "tomorrow" at 1281). Do not put its hands at the fateful four in that planning painting. At 1406–1408 show one clock reading four only if its dial is actually legible; otherwise retain the identifiable case and let the text carry the hour. No second clock and no Bellweir civic-clock design.
