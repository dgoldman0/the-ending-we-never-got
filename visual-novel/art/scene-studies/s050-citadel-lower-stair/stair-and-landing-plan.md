# Assault stair and entrance — working geometry

Prepared 25 September 2026 for S050/S052 and coordinated with the S055 producer. Root accepted this provisional arrangement for generation; actual S050 art must be inspected before it becomes the visual foundation. It supplements [the citadel plan](../chapter08-production/citadel-layout.md); compass labels and dimensions are practical production choices, not newly asserted source canon.

The incoming lower flight climbs north into a right-angle bend; the upper route turns east and climbs to Olan's inner landing and the hall. The incoming fighting throat is 1.7–1.8 m clear, so only two attackers can engage Mara abreast. The western passage joins the LOWER approach below that throat. It never opens behind Mara's defensive line. Approximately twelve ordinary steps on the lower flight and ten on the upper flight are a workable starting point, not a locked count. Each riser/tread must support an adult boot; avoid giant monument steps.

A three-sided shallow recess lies against the upper/north wall of the bend, behind the fighting line: about 3.0 m along the eastward route and 1.8 m deep. A continuous 1.7–1.8 m turning/carrying lane passes along its open southern edge. Solid side walls prevent the recess becoming a second attacking route. This is a narrow throat with a protected wall-side bay, not a plaza where six attackers can surround the defender. An upper gallery overlooks the approach and remains usable by the later reserve.

The bay permits Mara's roughly 1.76 m body to lie fully supported after the battle, head east/uphill and feet west/downhill, with Tessa, Elin and healer kneeling close while wounded can pass. This later body placement is provisional until actual S050 geometry is reviewed. Neither the fall nor a body move is invented as a depicted action. The defended bend remains the same place across promise, defense and grief; broken shields appear later.

Upper landing: approximately 5 m across by 4 m deep, clear of stored furniture. Upper stair enters from west; hall entrance occupies east wall. An upper side window has a real oblique sightline across to the defensive tower and its steps. Bronze double doors open inward into the rectangular hall, leaving the landing and threshold physically open; the separate black entrance ward spans the opening. Agreed clear doorway 3.2 m wide by 3.6 m high beneath a shallow segmental stone arch. Two plain heavy bronze leaves, each 1.6 m wide, have restrained recessed rectangular panels and open inward against the hall side cheeks. No magical mechanism is built into the doors; the black entrance ward is separate. First hall pillars stand beyond the 1.6 m leaf swing. Inner faces, hinge positions and jamb width must match the S054 hall view. This supersedes the earlier provisional 2.3×3.4 m opening. Two pillar rows and the distant throne belong to the hall, never an amphitheatre or circular chamber.

S050 base camera: modestly elevated from lower/southwest approach, looking toward the bend and east/uphill flight. Tessa and Mara embrace in the sheltered bay edge, Olan signals from above, and the narrow throat/turn/upper route remain legible. The western branch can be only partly visible; do not turn a useful establishing frame into a schematic cutaway. S050 guard key uses the same wall, floor and body positions at an earlier instant, with close enough hands to judge buckle mechanics.

S052 camera: from southwest side of upper landing toward the east doorway, with stair head at left/rear and high tower window on a real side wall. Olan faces the door with adapted RIGHT-arm shield between threat and companions; Tessa has practical reach to the north jamb from immediately behind/alongside the shield, Elin crouches within cover, and runner reaches the stair head. Their exact screen positions may adapt together after a whole-body proof; anatomical laterality, ward plane, stair orientation and sightline cannot change.

All scenes use worn gray stone, real wall thickness and practical supports, with predawn moving into gray dawn. Keep neutral readable source exposure. No rugs, new flags or decorative furniture. Upper route, bend and lower entry are one continuous assault route, separate from the infirmary court and later broad medical-wagon gate.
