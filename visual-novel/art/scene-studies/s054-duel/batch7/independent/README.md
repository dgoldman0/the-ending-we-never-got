## Actual GIMP work

The near-pillar room plate is `../13-empty-hall-near-pillar.png`. Valcair is the precisely traced, exact-pixel `batch7-cats-minor/duel-valcair-component` mask from `09-spearman-foundation.png`. Tessa is the exact `../tessa-component.png`. Neither actor was regenerated. A single rigid 9.7-degree rotation of her complete healthy glove, grip, guard and pommel around the wrist aligns the hilt with the separately masked real steel blade from the current throne-hall painting. The cuff is feathered locally; finger relationships remain unchanged. Valcair's arm and hand pixels are retained without body transforms. A scaled copy of his existing horn sits behind the crown to make the far horn readable.

Other separate layers contain the shortened real wooden shaft and metal spearhead, small bounded impact cracks/chips, contact/cast shadows, generated sanctuary-light texture in Screen mode, and sparse black ward texture in Multiply mode. The densest black patch sits on the actual blade/elbow crossing, with weaker patches at other joints. The black donor is first cropped in its own full-size image; this avoids losing its low-positioned dark knot to the shorter scene canvas during selection. No magic hides a missing limb chain.

All raster masks, transforms, blending and exports are executed by GIMP. Python builds the Scheme instructions and simple prop/detail SVGs, computes coordinates/hashes and makes inspection boards; it does not edit production raster pixels. Reproducible scripts are in `art/prompts/s054-duel/batch7/independent/`: `crop-black-ward.scm`, `build-final.py`, its generated `deterministic-final.scm`, and `export-delivery.scm`. Export groups all native layers and applies exactly the same scale factor, 1920/1672, to both axes. The 1920×1080 canvas trims half a native pixel from the bottom. A GIMP alpha-transfer mask restores full coverage at the interpolated rectangular background edge while preserving its sampled RGB. See `verification.json` for exact reopened pixels, opacity, dimensions and pre-promotion source hash, and `promotion.json` for delivery provenance.
