## Delivered lever / item 57: physically rebuilt three-step platform

The delivered master is **`s054-lever-final.xcf`** and its matching `s054-lever-final.png`. The earlier current composition merged the dais into the rear hall; merely painting three steps on one edge did not repair its elevation. Independent whole-scene review rejected that approach. The final composition moves collapsed Valcair to the visible lower right stair: his knee/legs rest against the lowest step and lower floor, his supporting hand bears weight on the low tread, and the other arm reaches upward toward the spear butt on the top landing. Exactly three risers connect that landing to the lower hall; the left retaining wall and bounded rear edge establish one platform. His broad swept-back horns, predominantly black hair with silver temples, warm brown complexion and dark armor retain his established identity.

Tessa continues using her bare LEFT hand on the release while her injured RIGHT glove stays at her chest. Three separate GIMP masks restore her current source facial/hair core, bloody right glove and left lever grip, using measured similarity transforms to fit the rebuilt composition. These are original source details, uniformly scaled/rotated/translated in GIMP; they are not claimed to retain byte-for-byte pixel equality after interpolation. The sword stays separate and the spear is reversed so the reaching hand approaches the butt, not the blade.

The former leaves the rear platform flush with the hall. `s054-rejected-trench-donor.png` is rejected because its rear stairs ascend into a trench around Valcair. The first `s054-dais-donor.png` and Valcair head donor/targets belong to that abandoned repair path. `s054-visible-step-donor.png` establishes the usable new blocking but still has the spear pointing at his fingers; `s054-spear-boundary-donor.png` fixes spear orientation and the rear boundary, then receives the three current-source restoration layers.

Root independently inspected the final whole image and native Tessa face/hands, Valcair face/both horns/arm chains, spear butt, step contacts and rear tile termination before clearing promotion. `s054-lever-verification.json` records source and final hashes, opaque dimensions, four meaningful layers and exact reopened output.
