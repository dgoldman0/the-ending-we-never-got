# Batch 7 citadel repairs

Work starts from the current scene PNGs after the other team's local repairs. The earlier layered masters are not used as current source authority. This is production review, not user approval or an in-game acceptance claim.

## Delivered first group: S051, both S052 paintings, S053

- **S051 / item 58:** remove the pale wrap only from Lucan's right knuckles above the crossbow stock. Exactly 370 source pixels change. The original left hand, sleeve/cuff, crossbow, face and every other pixel remain unchanged.
- **S052 pair / item 60:** continue matte stone through the narrow pilaster, threshold and visible hall floor; resolve the doubled right jamb; replace the retained glossy patches beside the runner's boots and Olan's boot. Separate masks preserve the original people and black ward. The entrance variant has its original bare left hand restored through a dedicated contour. A protected-region audit caught softened Olan curls from one tiny material patch; that patch was removed, restoring his original hair.
- **S053 / item 49:** fresh composition follows the dry landing, parapet and straight upper stair in current S050 and S055-Mara. Mara bars the approach with two attackers before her; her surviving soldier supports the wounded man up the flight behind her. Mara's sword is in her right hand and her plain round shield on her left arm. The first draft's reversed weapons are superseded by the masked hand donor. The attacker's complete blade and crossguard are retained separately; a generated third horn and its isolated fragment are removed. This is an intentionally new composition for the requested geography/blocking correction.

Prompts and reproducible GIMP scripts are in `../../prompts/batch7-citadel/`. The built-in image tool generated the S051 knuckle donor, S052 stone donor and S053 composition/hand donor. Each final has a meaningful layered XCF and an independently reopened flat PNG. `subgroup-1-verification.json` records original and candidate hashes, dimensions, opaque alpha, pixel comparisons and protected-region checks. Root independently inspected each whole painting and relevant native details before authorizing promotion. Runtime grading and play review remain for the other team.

Native S053 generation is 1672×941; delivery uses uniform scaling to 1920×1081 and one bottom-row crop, not an aspect-ratio stretch. The other delivered PNGs keep their original 1920×1080 canvas.
