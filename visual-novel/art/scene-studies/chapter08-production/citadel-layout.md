## Separate defensive and assault fronts

The west approach is the attacked gate; the river exit stays distinct and available for Marren/wife/brother to leave. A covered walkway wide enough for beds runs along the ward doors. Lucan can move from a firing slit at the west gate across the court to the laundry side without teleporting, while Serat replaces him at the vacated slit. Wards are limewashed inside gray stone, with deep window embrasures, simple beds, linen shelves and a small stove. At S045 Lucan’s treatment chair/bed is across the same ward from Marren’s bed; a single broad ward doorway later shows beds being moved away from windows in S047. Do not put all named people at one impossible bedside.

The assault route is on a different citadel face: lower exterior approach rises to a narrow TURNING stair, western passage meets at Mara’s bend, next flight reaches Olan’s inner landing and the throne-hall entrance. Do not use the infirmary gate/court as this bend or route. The stair’s fighting width near Mara is roughly1.6–1.8m, enough for two attackers abreast rather than a grand ballroom stair. A broad enough turning landing permits a wounded man to be carried uphill past the defender without stepping through a wall. Olan’s upper landing is about4m deep and5m wide, enough for his adapted shield, Tessa, Elin and subsequent wounded. An upper wall window has an oblique view toward a nearby defensive tower; it is not a decorative window facing solid interior masonry.

These proportions are practical production choices. If an actual image requires adjustment, update its neighboring views to match. Do not compress adult bodies, bedside furniture or stairs to fit a narrow frame.
