# Opening runtime art review — 16 September 2026

Status: **PRESENTATION REJECTED BY THE USER, 16 September 2026.** The earlier observations below are retained as a historical production record. They do not override the rejection or clear these assets for reuse. The [new audit](../../presentation-redesign.md) identifies failures in composition, performance, integration, rendering and lighting, alongside interface and discovery problems. Reassess components against selected references and the source; do not use this batch as an approved reference set.

The batch is restricted to S001–S005. The [manifest](manifest.json) retains each character's age, wardrobe, props, injury state, light and source bounds for that assessment. The screenplay remains unchanged.

## Actual production and corrections

Eight full-body transparent sprite masters cover five Tessa states and one opening state each for Mara, Iven and Senn. Nine backgrounds cover the opening arch, fallen groceries, closure, three apartment states, palace ward, audience hall and the later window conversation. The two existing tone studies were removed from runtime; their pixels are not the game's scene presentation.

The built-in image-generation tool produced the components. GIMP 2.10.36 performed alpha-mask cleanup, scene lighting, masked dress/door/chair repairs, twelve-ray emblem repairs, prop placement and native review crops. The checked-in Script-Fu tools reproduce the final masters and exports. Each XCF retains useful source/component/mask/light layers. Sprite bodies are painted images, not independently articulated anatomy; exposure curves are baked copies, not live adjustment filters.

An initial sprite export ignored transferred masks and produced opaque background rectangles. The exporter now applies the selected layer's alpha mask in a disposable image. The working XCF retains its editable mask. Actual dark-room and bright-room composites were reopened and inspected after the correction.

The generated apartment's offered dress initially stood like an empty torso. A masked replacement makes it limp fabric at the wardrobe. The door opens only after Mara enters, closes when she leaves, and the chair moves from the window to the door after the phone dies. The old window chair is removed in that state. The final room remains very dark; cold window light and a small candle pool leave expressions and the phone readable.

The first grocery-in-background edit was rejected: its placement straddled the threshold and its milk outlet was wrong. A fresh isolated prop has a closed folded top and a torn bottom seam connected to the puddle. GIMP places that component one step inside the arch, at the same coordinates before and after closure. The bag is not at the room's center. The open arch's Earth view has its own mask so temple grading does not turn its fluorescent shop, asphalt and concrete into warm fantasy architecture.

Tessa's ceremony badge and the hall banners each have twelve short rays. Senn's inconsistent generated embroidery was replaced only inside costume masks, with the repairs constrained to his original silhouette. His face and hands remain from the original sprite. The repair plate is retained separately; it was not used as a new full-character reference.

## Manual visual observations

- **Tessa, nineteen:** compared against the selected storm-infirmary face crop. The expressive straight brows, ordinary eye size, nose, mouth, warm complexion and chestnut hair remain recognizable. Arrival versions have neither later injury nor late-campaign wear. Full-body views retain ordinary thigh/lower-leg proportions; face, hands, phone grip and shoes were inspected at native resolution. The long working robe and full lapis/celadon ceremony outfit are distinct complete costumes. The ceremony expression reads as refusal, not cheerful consent. These are limited early-state expressions, not a comprehensive acting set.
- **Mara, thirty-two:** full figure, face and hands inspected. Strong jaw, low dark braid, slate military construction and one captain's bar remain readable. The indoor pose has no drawn weapon or unexplained shield. Her silhouette and hair edges hold against the very dark apartment.
- **Iven, twenty-six:** full figure, face and broad hands inspected. Black curls, long face, medium-brown skin, olive coat and washable apron distinguish him from Senn. He looks toward the treatment/window area in these close dialogue views. This is not a travel, Harrow or later grief sprite.
- **Senn, late fifties:** full figure, beard/face, both hands and repaired embroidery inspected. The older priest is identified in the dialogue; he is not Lucan. No human emblem is placed on a northern character in this batch.
- **Light:** the final in-game summoning and ceremony captures show severe exposure across both people and the room, including faces and gestures. It is not an isolated glow on Tessa or merely a white floor. Ordinary infirmary/window light is cooler and insistent but less severe. Night preserves large areas of deep darkness. Dialogue stays readable on an opaque panel.
- **Framing:** opening dialogue views crop lower legs. Treatment, sleeve-grip and seated-window conversations use closer portraits with the relevant hands/lower body outside the frame. Portraits disappear on narrated action. The backgrounds are location plates, not complete frozen tableaux of every action in the prose.
- **Final exports:** full background exports, opening in-game composites, title, ceremony, apartment, moved-chair state and later-window view inspected after GIMP work. Hair masks have no opaque rectangles or visible outer halos in the inspected composites. The corrected room states retain perspective and prop location through changes.

All **17 XCF files** were reopened in GIMP and their visible layers exported again. Numerical comparisons on both white and black backgrounds found an exact visible-pixel and alpha match with the nine delivered backgrounds and eight neutral sprite masters. This reproducibility check supplements, and does not replace, the manual observations above.

## Coverage limits

This is a usable first set of portraits and environments, not completed scene animation/CG production. The running, lever operation, scholar/guard/crowd staging, wet footwear, cloak delivery/discard, candle carriage, Olan's missing digits and treatment effects still need their own action components. Orra and the other supporting opening cast need sprites. Do not label these omissions as a complete summoning tableau or solve them with a convenient unrelated portrait.

The ordinary ceremony costume is retained into the later infirmary conversation, but its angry face is only a limited early-state acting pose; a quieter reaction and seated action variant remain useful next production tasks. No mirrored pose is used to fake a changed facing direction.

S006–S058 do not reuse this batch. Winter, intermediate campaign, late injuries, postwar physical/emotional wear and growing children remain explicit work. Unused light exports are technical variants, not permission to deploy a character in another scene. The unused Bellweir heron study is outside runtime and has not completed its quality pass.
