# Institutional emblems — fixed geometry

These references implement the existing [prop designs](../../../visual-continuity.md). They separate the human temple's brass sun from the northern split white star. The user's correction to Lucan's first undivided badge prompted the local repair; it did not create a new faction symbol.

512 × 512 geometry references constructed and reviewed in GIMP. These are shape/color guides, not finished embroidered fabric or a seal impression. Preserve the gap at scene scale. Use the opaque previews as generation references so an alpha-discarding preview cannot hide the northern division. The templates retain real transparency; the northern center column is transparent. Both XCF exports were reopened and checked against their PNGs.

Tessa's and Lucan's character keys have separately editable local emblem and cloth-repair layers beneath their exposure copies. Maintain perspective and material treatment when fitting these marks to future clothes. Do not convert every star, civilian garment or horned character into an institutional badge, and keep Vask's seal distinct from the royal seal.
