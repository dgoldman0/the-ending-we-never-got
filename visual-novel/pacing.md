# Narrative time and room for investigation

Implementation update: the first playable route retains every screenplay action and spoken line, including its existing time skips. The additional compression below remains a proposal; automated traversal duration is not a measured human reading time.

**Current interaction decision:** [Look closer / Threads](investigation.md) replaces the rejected card/quiz design. References below to investigation and rounds mean optional reader discovery at candidate story spans, not actions performed by the player as a character or five compulsory menu interruptions.

Status: FIRST PACING PROPOSAL. The user asked to make room for investigation by considering narrative time skips. Rebuild 11 already contains substantial jumps and two montage headings. Preserve that compression where the intervening days do not contain a necessary interaction; do not turn every elapsed week into playable errands.

**Story time and reading time are separate.** Ten minutes spent investigating does not leave Tessa standing at a bridge for ten minutes or cause a missed rescue. A time card advances the world only at an authored transition. Investigation is outside that clock.

## Where to reduce presentation, not meaning

S029 and S043 already have montage form. They should remain concise narrative sequences rather than become hubs with a task for every visit or settlement. Short travel bridges around S024/S026 and between established dates can carry elapsed time without repeating negotiations. The three-day wharf stand-off needs its changing leverage, not three playable days.

For S043, a source-faithful compression could have this shape; the actual narration is still to be drafted:

1. **Spring:** Tessa clears the curse-cloud; Mara helps her stand. Cut to Lucan refused at the upper stores, with Marren visible but inaccessible.
2. **Following summer:** the village is already empty; Tessa puts away her drawing. Cut to Marren's wife receiving her unopened parcel.
3. **Winter:** southern flags rise and the stores prepare to retreat. Lucan's hired boat is under repair; his boatman brings the transfer news, Serat sends for Marren's wife, and the next full scene is the extraction.

The boatman's actionable news remains a short live exchange. This preserves why the next attempt differs from the failed ones. It also shows time through people, places, repairs and absence, without adding a new battle or explaining grief in a speech.

These choices mostly prevent expansion that a VN could otherwise accumulate. The screenplay is already economical; this is not a claim that several hours can be cut from a measured build.

## Scenes to give room

The training meal, inn key, first Bellweir market, boat outing, Hest/Orren reunion and laughing portrait establish affection and ordinary life. They should not become the first material cut to make room for lore. Likewise, preserve the hearing, the source's rescue mechanics, the coat scene, mill-town aftermath, Mara's last embrace and the final meal's invitation. Optional investigation should deepen a relationship the player has experienced, not replace it with a biography.

The original route's mandatory information stays in the story even when a related investigation can examine it more closely. Marren's testimony cannot become optional merely because R05 compares the evidence. The historical investigation may reveal additional truth without becoming required homework before the player can understand the ending.

## Reading rhythm

The five [candidate discovery spans](investigation.md) end at settled boundaries: the first window-seat scene, the hearing's corridor, the exchange negotiations, the seasonal sequence and the final preparations. Use them to plan availability while designing scene-level opportunities to Look closer. Keep entry optional and connections revisitable through Threads. Do not interrupt Iven's death, the coat scene, the mill-town cart exchange, the stair assault or the bakery ending with an unlock notice or force a full-screen checkpoint at every boundary.

Measure the first playable chapter both straight through and with R01 completed. Track dialogue, action/narration, transitions and investigation separately. Only then estimate the full game's reading time. The current optional investigation allowance is a design target, not grounds to shorten every emotional scene to fit an untested total runtime.
