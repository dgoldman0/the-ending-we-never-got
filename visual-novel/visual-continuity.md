# Original VN — objects, effects and visual continuity

Status: WORKING DESIGN, 16 September 2026. Companion to the [character designs](../characters/original-visuals.md) and [locations](../worldbuilding/original-locations.md). These are physical design decisions and checks for future assets, not generated art or an art-style choice. Rebuild 11 remains unchanged.

## Magic as visible action

Use the source's physical behavior and colors regardless of eventual rendering style.

- **Purification:** Tessa's white light passes over visible dark corruption and leaves ordinary tissue. Healing does not grow Olan's fingers back. The cleansing light does not permanently bleach skin or change her appearance.
- **Healing:** the older healer uses blue light; Tessa can close surviving wound edges. Serat's central gouge remains and still needs surgical care. Other healers' professional actions remain visible.
- **Sanctuary:** a white curve or pane with a comprehensible extent; the audience must see who is underneath, where debris lands and what becomes exposed when Tessa stretches it. A maintained effect can persist when her hands do something else. It is not a rigid glass object with unexplained sharp shards.
- **Friendly ward:** Mara's blue shoulder protection remains intact beneath sanctuary. Tessa deliberately touches and removes it in training; incidental proximity is not dispelling.
- **Northern protection and barriers:** black light has visible edges against its surroundings and can be renewed. Valcair's armor joints become exposed metal when cleared. A dark surface need not hide his limbs, weapon position or the stone floor from the viewer.
- **Foundation sabotage:** black grains among white ward salt precede dark housings, crystal growth and actual structural collapse. Destruction continues after a ward fails because roof and masonry have lost support. No magical wiring, cuffs or silver hoops are added.
- **Summoning:** the opening briefly shows the real street, contracts and disappears. Subsequent chamber views retain the damaged arch. No ambient portal remains available for a player to open by clicking it.

## Player investigation and visible knowledge

An investigative close view, recovered account or contextual explanation may teach the **player** more than the cast knows. It does not automatically become an item Tessa carries, a document Elin has read or a conclusion Lucan can state. The player may learn humanity initiated the ancient war; the existing original cast's ignorance remains in effect.

This pass supplies ordinary objects and places for a coherent world. It does not approve a particular historical artifact, event, inscription, discovery order or investigation interface. Avoid casually decorating inhabited rooms with a readable account of the ancient aggression and then claiming no resident knows it. Historical evidence can be designed deliberately in the investigation pass.

## Source issue to resolve before final prop lettering

In the throne hall, Tessa says **“Your seal was on the bridge.”** Earlier staging and Elin's testimony identify **Vask's seal** on the wagon clearance. The source does not establish an identical royal impression on that clearance. This is a wording/attribution question for an interaction-level review; do not silently solve it by forging a new document, merging the two seal designs, or claiming Tessa saw Valcair's private order. No screenplay text has been changed here.
