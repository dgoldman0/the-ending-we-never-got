# Working instructions

## Canonical manuscript

Edit `original-timeline/source.fountain`. The current baseline is Staged Rebuild 11. Preserve the existing story unless the user explicitly authorizes a story change. Use Git commits for actual future changes rather than adding numbered revision directories. This branch intentionally contains only the latest source and essential text notes; do not resume the abandoned transport or history-import workflow.

## Editing method

Work on one connected interaction at a time, including its necessary action. Read the full scene and check downstream dependencies before accepting the edit. Keep a larger reading window than drafting window. Do not generate a wholesale replacement screenplay. The user does not need to approve each internal micro-edit.

Before editing an exchange, identify what each person knows, wants, fears and is trying to obtain from the other person. Record which outcome and later dependencies must stay fixed. Diagnose a specific defect, try local alternatives, choose a revision and reread it with adjacent material. After local work, perform a separate connected-scene regression pass.

## Blocking editorial gates

A revised passage is not automatically cleared. Every applicable gate needs passage-specific evidence tied to the exact candidate. Missing review, unresolved defects or stale evidence block final PDF delivery. A layout check, word count, number of passes or pending actor read cannot override an identifiable writing failure.

1. **Responsive dialogue.** Replies arise from what the character heard, observed, misunderstood or deliberately avoided. Do not write alternating statements of what the audience needs to know. Honest answers and shared enjoyment remain available; deflection is not mandatory.
2. **Varied conversational rhythm.** Avoid both serial clipped declarations and routine paragraph-length briefings. Length and syntax follow the speaker's immediate purpose. Do not mechanically add interruptions, ellipses, slang or conjunctions.
3. **Breathing room.** Important reactions, silence and ongoing activity can register without explanatory microblocking. Retain movements that matter to attention, action or relationship; remove gestures used merely to punctuate each line.
4. **Clear, economical phrasing.** Concrete verbs and available referents. No forced wit, undefined objects deciding outcomes, duplicated mechanics lessons or recaps of action just shown.
5. **Distinct voice.** Preserve personality, relationship, expertise and circumstances. Avoid giving everyone the same polished explanation, therapeutic interpretation or military report. Direct admissions such as 'I want Iven' can be earned.
6. **Emotional consequence.** Suspicion, anger, grief and affection affect behavior and later encounters. Separate factual evidence, practical cooperation, personal access and forgiveness.
7. **Literal staging.** Trace consequential starting states, movement and outcomes: doors, occupied hands, weapons, documents, boats, geography and injuries. Allow ordinary inference for inconsequential motion.
8. **Knowledge and capabilities.** Do not leak audience knowledge, erase professional competence or forget demonstrated powers. Spell limits must be intelligible before they determine a loss.
9. **Earned decisions and resistant world.** Cooperation, access, discoveries and rescue depend on meaningful attempts and others' interests. Avoid convenient paperwork, witnesses or permission appearing merely because the plot needs them. Keep opponents adaptive and Lucan independently effective.
10. **Protected story.** No unannounced changes to chronology, magic, promises, trust, identity or outcomes in a language pass.
11. **Attention and duration.** Preserve character life and quiet; do not let procedural explanation dominate. The original approximately thirty-minute intention remains an allocation concern, not a hard cap or a measured runtime. Rebuild 11's delivered reading copy had 57 screenplay pages plus a title page.
12. **Coverage and integration.** Review every source page, every changed interaction and all affected adjacent/dependent material. Further edits reopen the relevant checks.
13. **Export integrity.** A final PDF must match reviewed source and the editable document, with every final page inspected for layout defects. Export verification does not establish artistic perfection.

Independent cold reading, actor/table reading and timed performance were not performed for the current draft. Do not invent those tests or use their absence to excuse a visible source defect.

## Protected project decisions

- Original Iven and Mara are native characters without prior-Earth memories or VN knowledge. Only their altered-timeline counterparts are reincarnated fans from Neri's original reality, from around the era of her early life. They were born and grew up in the fantasy world.
- Tessa uses American English. Preserve the milk carton splitting and her mother's ordinary text message at the summoning.
- Senn promised a return without a verified method. Elin continues searching. Tessa has not rejected an available route home by choosing Bellweir over temple accommodation.
- Lucan does not know the buried history of humanity initiating the war. That revelation is absent from this opening. Reliable deeper VN lore may be known by the reincarnated fans in the altered timeline.
- Gray Scar's shared danger is outside both armies' command. Cooperation is bounded; Lucan's voluntary return for the last litter matters.
- Harrow ruptures Tessa's trust in Lucan. She suspects him, rejects contact, and receives later evidence through intermediaries. Evidence and tactical cooperation do not automatically restore intimacy.
- Lucan has costly, independent agency; Elin's and his investigations encounter resistance. Marren admits his own complicity but cannot certify everything Tessa wants to know.
- The original victory remains brutal and bittersweet: Iven and Mara die, Tessa sustains a lasting hand injury, peace becomes possible and she has a worthwhile future while still seeking home.
- Neri's altered timeline stays untouched here. Neri's age, immense familiar company, playful enthusiasm and overwhelmingly positive transformation belong to that later story; do not import her temperament into original-route Tessa.
