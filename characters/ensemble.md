# Supporting cast and relationship boundaries

The [original-timeline visual designs](original-visuals.md) provide new working appearances, clothing and injury continuity for this cast, including recurring civilians and smaller roles. They supplement the relationships below without adopting altered-route memories or biographies.

## Iven Saye

Current original draft: twenty-six at introduction; experienced healer, Tessa's professional ally and friend. His occupation does not exhaust his personality. Checkers, shared meals and the way he makes room for her matter. He dies rescuing Renn at Harrow.

Altered identity: a fan from Neri's original reality, who died young before agelessness and was born as Iven. He has grown up in this world and really is a healer. Memories of the game do not replace his local life. He and Mara already cooperate before Tessa arrives. His foreknowledge of Iven's canonical death gives the bridge a personal future he must live toward.

Earlier altered-draft family: brother Dann, a woodworker; Iven wants a recovery-ward veranda where patients can sit outside. Commissioning work for a date after his expected death gives survival a concrete future. These details are recovered draft material, not additions to the original screenplay.

## Mara Venn

Current original draft: thirty-two, a captain and initially Tessa's coercive escort. She becomes a trainer and close attachment through choices, not an apology alone. Protecting Lucan's promised departure has professional consequences. She mediates evidence after Harrow and dies holding the citadel stair.

Altered identity: a reincarnated fan with her own local upbringing, career and family. She has clearer source knowledge than Neri and can prepare within real professional constraints. She must not become a newly arrived confused player at the summoning or automatically command every institution.

Earlier altered family: sister Ness and niece Pet, with family sewing, birthdays and a calendar commitment. Surviving to attend Pet's birthday and later walk the formerly fatal stair with family gives the positive timeline lived consequences. Eren appears as a colleague covering a duty shift; this is a minor draft role, not a developed cast commitment.

## Lucan — Demon Prince

Current name replaces Cael/Kael. He does not know humanity's buried initiating atrocity. His father's distrust can shape his upbringing without giving him its complete factual explanation.

His rescue at Gray Scar is voluntary after his own people are safe. Current development also gives him costly searches, transport bargains, moral compromises, resistance from officials, the loss of his boat, a dangerous witness extraction and independent defense of the infirmary. He must remain a person with competence, ambition, pleasure and faults rather than someone who petitions his father and forwards paperwork.

Tessa challenges a transport bargain supporting quarry labor. His effectiveness is compatible with misjudgment; trusting his father helps expose people at Harrow. Her later suspicion and refusal of intimacy survive practical cooperation. He cannot take Tessa's climactic victory away from her.

Earlier altered draft: riding, boot-lining, translating letters, a southern visit and eventual romance. Those scene ideas are reusable only after restoring his current individuality and the changed causes. His appearance now has a [working original-VN design](original-visuals.md#lucan); the altered romantic course remains open.

## Valcair — Demon King

A ruler and father with a present strategic objective: river access, control of forts and elimination of the summoning threat. His historical distrust has a deeper cause withheld from the original opening. He knowingly chooses atrocities; love for Lucan is selective and does not excuse them.

Working visual: black horns swept back, silver-streaked hair, armor protected by black light, spear. These are draft visuals rather than a complete species guide.

Original endpoint: Tessa kills him. Altered endpoint: his schemes become ineffective, assumptions fail and involvement in a different life makes war cease to interest him. The old Brim/model-boat encounter is a seed for personal change, not a sufficient conversion by itself.

## Vask

Northern general. Capable of proposing alternatives but ultimately a knowing participant in Harrow. Her choices need personal and institutional pressures; a signature cannot substitute for a moral transition. She obstructs the later inquiry and has forces of her own. Original ceasefire is useful but does not absolve her. Her altered future needs explicit treatment alongside the king's.

## Elin Orr

Twenty-two in the original introduction; scholar, investigator and friend. Current discovery of the return deception comes through discrepancies, risky access and questioning a reluctant participant. She loses archive access and obtains limited help, not every resource she wants. She continues to seek a route home.

She has no prior-Earth or VN knowledge in either route unless separately authorized. She is not among the three people who learn Neri's full former history. The older altered draft's cheerful technical curiosity remains a working trait, not permission for unrestricted knowledge.

## Senn and Orra

Prelate Senn is responsible for summoning and deceptive assurances. Need, institutional self-protection and control shape his conduct. He does not have a verified way to send Tessa back.

Marshal Orra has military authority and needs effective care, but she is not an unlimited benevolent override for every obstruction. Current concessions remain bounded. Both should have specific interests rather than interchangeable exposition duties.

## Olan, Serat and Marren

Olan is the first important saved patient. Missing fingers remain; an adapted shield grip later enables independent participation in Tessa's campaign. He protects others rather than existing only to prove her kindness.

Serat is Lucan's officer, wounded during the voluntary rescue and later capable of an independent command refusal. His brother participates in rescue/escort work. Loyalty develops from experience, not a speech that automatically mobilizes every captain.

Marren is a frightened, culpable stores clerk. In the current draft Lucan must reach and protect him and his wife. He signed a clearance without a proper reinspection. He did not see the charges being installed and cannot establish everything about Lucan's prior knowledge. His testimony is not a magically complete answer.

## Bellweir household

Hest and Orren run the bakery; Ada and Renn are their children; the apprentices matter to Orren and later return. Hest has agency in grief, prison visits and aid to searchers. The sapling-carrying northern family provides another particular civilian loss and a later recruitment consequence.

## Earlier altered-only local cast

Eda and Jory Vale are Neri's parents; Lark is her childhood friend and skiff collaborator. Jo Rusk is Tessa's mother. Dann, Ness and Pet supply Iven and Mara with family futures. Basil, Bellweir's senior summoner with twelve armored spirits, is a working contrast figure for the scale and coordination of Neri's company. Harven is an earlier-draft compromised quartermaster associated with the salt plot. These roles remain available but have not been harmonized with every Rebuild 11 event.
