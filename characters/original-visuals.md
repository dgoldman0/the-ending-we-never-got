# Original timeline — character appearance and wardrobe

Status: WORKING DESIGN, 16 September 2026. This fills visual gaps for the original-timeline VN. **Source anchors** come from Rebuild 11; **working designs** are new choices made for this adaptation, available for revision. They are not recovered approved artwork. Descriptions specify people, garments and physical continuity; the VN's current tone and reference treatment are recorded in [visual direction](../visual-novel/art/visual-direction.md). Heights are approximate guides for shared scenes, not required on-screen facts. Unspecified ages below describe apparent age only.

## Tessa Rusk

**Visual likeness reference:** the user selected Tessa in the [north-infirmary storm image](../visual-novel/art/scene-studies/north-infirmary/north-infirmary-storm.png) as the appearance to retain. A [face crop](../visual-novel/art/character-references/tessa/north-infirmary-face.png) isolates that reference. Preserve her facial structure and the painting's treatment across new scenes. For arrival, render the same person at nineteen, healthy and in her modern clothes; do not carry the infirmary injury, dirt, exhaustion or field costume backward. Later failed summoning studies are not likeness references.

**Source anchors:** nineteen at arrival; American; right-handed drawing before the final injury; coat, temple badge and white mantle; sneakers at summoning; soft right-hand brace at the four-year endpoint. Her appearance beyond these details was not established.

**Working design:** about 165 cm, ordinary build, becoming stronger through walking, rowing and training. Light warm-brown skin; an oval face with a broad expressive mouth, straight brows and dark-brown eyes. Chestnut-brown, slightly wavy hair falls just below her shoulders. She usually gathers it into a low ponytail for work, leaving shorter strands at her temples. Her face remains recognizable at twenty-three; changed bearing and daily habits carry the elapsed years. A small uneven front tooth is visible when she laughs. No magical change to her eyes or hair accompanies becoming a Saint.

**Range:** ready laughter, absorbed observation while drawing, impatient questioning, awkward pleasure at attention, controlled anger, exhaustion and grief. Her original-route warmth is distinct from Neri's exuberance. Match the period: early warmth is not the default for the postwar key. The user explicitly requires visible aging and battering from war by the endpoint—drawn facial volume, tired eyes, weathered skin, premature tension lines, neglected grooming and a weary, protective body. She remains chronologically about twenty-three and recognizably Tessa; preserving her identity must not freeze her at nineteen. These are working visual consequences, not additional named injuries or a change to the screenplay.

## Iven Saye

**Source anchors:** twenty-six at introduction; healer; hair sticks up after sleeping in camp; medical bag and coat; torn sleeve after Harrow.

**Working design:** about 179 cm, long-limbed with broad hands and a slight softness around the middle. Medium-brown skin, a long face, wide nose, brown eyes and thick black curls cropped at the sides but unruly on top. Usually clean-shaven, with stubble during travel. One eyebrow rises before he commits to teasing someone; his amusement can be quiet or expansive.

**Clothing:** open-neck linen shirt, charcoal trousers, olive knee-length coat with deep side pockets, laced boots. A washable apron is added for treatment and removed for meals. His brown medical satchel has a broad woven strap and a replaced pale fastening tab. Winter adds a heavy short cloak and knitted scarf. At Harrow he wears the olive coat, with no over-cape obstructing the sleeve Tessa catches. Working continuity assigns the torn sleeve to his right arm. Later scenes must not give living characters this coat as casual costume; Tessa's handling of it is a specific grief scene. His bag is separately present before the final assault.

## Mara Venn

**Source anchors:** thirty-two; captain; sword, shield, forearm guards and a blue shoulder ward; a coat familiar enough for Tessa to bury her face in it; dies on the citadel stair.

**Working design:** about 176 cm, solid through shoulders and legs, medium olive-brown skin. Squared jaw, slightly heavy eyelids, hazel eyes, thick dark-brown hair braided and pinned low against the nape. Strong nose with a modest bend, without assigning it an untold battle history. Her face can relax into an unguarded grin around the checkerboard. She should read as an adult in her thirties, not an elderly veteran.

**Clothing:** slate-blue military coat over a padded vest, practical trousers and tall boots. Ordinary mail and removable shoulder protection for deployments; training uses the padded layer. A narrow brass captain's bar sits at the collar. Her left arm carries the shield and her right hand the sword. Working placement for the blue ward is the left shoulder. The forearm guard Tessa fastens before the assault is the right one. Off duty she removes armor and loosens the coat; she does not sleep or eat every meal in full equipment. Her coat accumulates mended seams across the years but remains the same recognizable garment at the final embrace.

## Elin Orr

**Source anchors:** twenty-two; scholar; satchel, translations, coat and lamp; ultimately uses her sleeve to bind Tessa's injured hand.

**Working design:** about 160 cm, compact build, deep-brown skin, round cheeks tapering to a small chin, dark eyes and tightly curled black hair secured in a short twist at the back. An escaping curl at the forehead is a consistent hair feature, not a repeated gesture. Her hands carry occasional ink traces on their sides. She has an alert, sociable face rather than a permanent squint over books.

**Clothing:** ocher-brown wool jacket, dark teal skirt over practical underlayers, sturdy flat boots, pale linen blouse. The satchel has a stiff board backing so pages survive travel. In the field she wears trousers and a knee-length rain cloak instead of the skirt. At the citadel the pale blouse has sleeves broad enough to tear a useful strip; that sleeve remains damaged in the subsequent stair and infirmary sequence. No modern eyeglasses or magical reading aid are required by this design.

## Lucan

**Source anchors:** appears mid-twenties; northern prince; field seal, sword and crossbow; loses his command; wounded forearm during Marren's extraction, later torn coat and guarded ribs.

**Working design:** about 185 cm, lean but strong across the upper back. Warm medium-brown skin, a long face with a broad mouth, gray-green eyes, and near-black hair worn to the jaw, tied back when working aboard a boat. A pair of smooth dark horns curves back close to the skull, shorter than Valcair's. This horn design is a new family resemblance, not a previously established species rule. Otherwise humanlike proportions, ears, hands and feet; no added wings, tail or glowing eyes. Similarity to his father comes through brow, nose and horn direction, not identical faces.

**Clothing:** dark bottle-green riding coat over a pale shirt, fitted charcoal trousers, scuffed tall boots and an offset crossbow strap. A small split-star badge identifies his royal affiliation. Better tailoring distinguishes his status more than excessive jewelry. Losing the field seal and command pennant does not erase his family identity or require removing every badge. Later camp visits use rolled sleeves and an unfastened coat; his formal pose for Tessa's drawing contrasts with how he looks while laughing.

Working injury placement: **left forearm**, with the sleeve cut back for the dressing. At the citadel the wrap remains visible and the coat is torn. A rib injury is suggested by the source's guarded posture without inventing a precise diagnosis. The dry coat his father offers is separate and remains behind when Lucan leaves.

## Valcair

**Source anchors:** black horns swept back; silver-streaked hair; armored, with black protective light; spear. Exact chronological age is not established.

**Working design:** appears in his early fifties; about 193 cm, broad chest, powerful forearms and a face weathered around the eyes. Medium-brown skin like Lucan's, dark gray-green eyes, black hair with substantial silver at the temples and through the crown. Hair reaches the shoulders and is bound at the nape in the field. His horns sweep back above the ears, ridged near their roots, with neither a broken horn nor a historically meaningful scar added. A short, close beard distinguishes his lower face from Lucan's.

**Clothing:** charcoal wool, dark red lining and a heavy plain clasp in private rooms. Field and throne-hall armor is darkened steel with articulated joints; black ward-light seals those joints, visibly separate from the actual metal. His spear is a functional long weapon with a leaf-shaped head and a two-handed shaft. A narrow silver circlet is available for formal appearances but is absent from the final fight. Quiet concern for his son and deliberate cruelty must both be possible within the same face.

## Vask

**Source anchors:** northern general; commands engineers, carries orders and controls a seal; armor specifics and appearance are unestablished.

**Working design:** appears mid-forties; about 180 cm, broad, muscular build, tawny skin, a rectangular face and deep-set brown eyes. Iron-gray hair is cropped short around compact dark horns that curve outward then back. These horn shapes are individual working designs, not a rank marker. The weathering at her eyes and mouth is ordinary age and exposure; no disfigurement marks her culpability.

**Clothing:** dark plum field coat beneath steel shoulder and torso protection, black belt carrying a flat dispatch wallet and a seal case. Boots are repaired but maintained. The general's insignia is three narrow silver bars beneath a split-star pin. She wears a lighter vest indoors; field gloves come off to handle the lists and clearance. The seal and signed documents need distinct prop designs—her authority must not be visually confused with Valcair's.

## Senn and Orra

**Senn — working design:** late fifties, about 173 cm, rounded build, light-brown skin, thinning sandy-gray hair neatly brushed back, brown eyes and a carefully tended short beard. His public smile can be patient and convincing; no permanent sneer. Cream outer robe over a wheat-colored under-robe, sunburst embroidery limited to collar and cuffs, polished brass clasp. A charcoal indoor robe replaces ceremonial layers for private work. Rings are ordinary personal ornaments, not magical controls. The temple badge pinned to Tessa belongs to this institution's design vocabulary.

**Orra — working design:** early fifties, about 171 cm, sturdy build, dark-brown skin, close-cropped graying hair, dark eyes and a full, unsmiling resting mouth that can soften in ordinary company. Deep blue military coat with three brass collar bars, riding trousers and worn leather gloves. A short weather cloak and practical breastplate are added in the field. Her clothing shares Mara's military construction but indicates a different rank. She arrives at the hearing from the convoy yard; outdoor dust and gloves matter more than ceremonial grandeur.

Neither design requires a new age, rank system or ancestry to be spoken in the story. The insignia are working visual distinctions within the existing chain of command.

## Olan, Serat and Marren

**Olan — source anchors:** two missing fingers on the right hand; later an adapted shield grip. **Working design:** late twenties, about 175 cm, stocky, light tan skin, close-cut auburn hair, gray eyes and a square freckled face. Assign the missing digits to the right ring and little fingers for consistent drawings. The remaining thumb, index and middle fingers remain useful. His shield has a broad forearm strap and padded grip adapted for that right hand; it must not be a standard left-arm shield mirrored between scenes. A plain blue-gray padded soldier's coat, repaired mail and brown boots replace his initial infirmary shirt. The adaptation specifies the arrangement, not a medical explanation beyond the source.

**Serat — source anchors:** Lucan's lieutenant; thigh gouged at Gray Scar; splint, then walking stick; later returns to active defense. **Working design:** early thirties, about 181 cm, broad-built, dark umber skin, black close-cropped hair, amber-brown eyes, blunt nose and short horns rising close to the temples. Dark green padded uniform, steel shoulder guards, plain split-star badge. Working injury side is the **left thigh**. Use the splint in early recovery, then a stick as written; later movement can retain stiffness without inventing permanent incapacity. No fresh open wound in the late citadel scenes.

**Marren — source anchors:** stores clerk; carries documents, loses dry clothing during escape; signed clearance is central. **Working design:** early forties, about 168 cm, slight build, pale brown skin, narrow face, thinning dark hair and brown eyes. Small rounded horns are visible above the ears. Faded brown clerk's coat, cream shirt, narrow cuffs, ink-marked fingers and a modest leather document wallet. His wet coat is pressed into the leaking hull; the testimony scene uses dry borrowed clothing. Fear alters his posture without making his everyday design look inherently dishonest.

Horned northern designs are a working visual population choice. They do not establish that every northern resident shares one ancestry, that all horned people support Valcair, or that “demon” is a complete biological classification. Clothing adaptations around horns must still be wearable; helmets and hoods need openings or room rather than intersecting bodies.

## Shared continuity rules

Keep bodily left and right fixed across poses. Expressions and clothing may vary; injuries, horn shape, hair part, facial structure and dominant equipment hands do not flip with the side of the screen. Record changes of clothes and treatment rather than accumulating every previous state on one default figure. Blood, wetness, dust, grief and fatigue each have their own scene context. Four years of story require growing children, seasonal layers and wear, not a new face for every adult.
