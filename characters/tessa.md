# Tessa Rusk

## Original heroine

Nineteen when summoned, roughly twenty-three at the four-year endpoint. She is an ordinary girl taken from Earth without consent, not an enthusiastic volunteer for an adventure. Her Saint role is imposed before she can understand the world or its war.

Her American voice and ordinary errand are intentional. The carton splits on the summoning-room floor while her mother's text asks whether she got the milk. That contrast is disturbing and personal, not a broad slapstick routine.

Tessa's original appeal is earnest, particular and emotionally accessible. She draws, remembers home through images, develops loyalties, enjoys company and has preferences that can inconvenience others. She acquires genuine expertise and makes consequential choices. Avoid reducing her to compassion plus suffering, or making her original demeanor whimsical simply because the eventual altered story is fun.

## Current trajectory

She discovers that her purification can stop curses ordinary healers cannot remove. Institutions convert that success into demands. Mara's early confinement is a real betrayal of autonomy; later closeness does not erase it. Iven provides professional support and lived companionship.

Bellweir and Hest's family give her an adopted community without replacing the mother she wants to reach. Elin's investigation exposes that Senn promised a return without knowing how to provide it. Tessa secures greater control over her work; she does not acquire a usable route home.

Gray Scar forces bounded cooperation with Lucan's party. Their respect and tentative attraction develop through specific actions, disagreements and visits. Harrow then makes his assurances intolerable: Iven dies and Tessa suspects Lucan himself. Evidence received through intermediaries does not instantly restore trust.

After Harrow, she continues helping people and learns to kill under pressure. Iven's absence changes how later experiences are borne. She centrally defeats Valcair while Mara dies holding the stair. Her drawing hand sustains lasting damage. The original conclusion allows companionship, useful life and an unfinished search for home without undoing those losses.

## Altered possibilities

Neri's interventions should make room for Tessa to live beyond compelled service. She may learn, heal, lead, explore, choose relationships, disagree and enjoy ordinary things without paying the original sacrificial price.

The earlier draft restores a connection to her mother and makes later medical travel voluntary. Tessa can ask for days at home, keep plans with her mother and return for reasons of her own. This is a developed proposal, not a capability established in the current original opening.

A successful romance with Lucan is available in the altered story. It requires something she enjoys about this particular person and choices they make together; gratitude and shared patient lists are not enough. Neri's excitement should not overwrite Tessa's privacy or choices.

The late discovery that her life was fiction to her friends is personal, not just explanatory. She can object to information withheld from her and decline to watch her original suffering. Her gratitude need not erase that objection.

## Working design and props

For the original VN, the user-selected likeness is the central woman in [north-infirmary-storm.png](../visual-novel/art/scene-studies/north-infirmary/north-infirmary-storm.png), also isolated in the [face reference](../visual-novel/art/character-references/tessa/north-infirmary-face.png). Preserve her identity and naturalistic painted treatment across scenes. The selection anchors her face; it does not approve the image's costume as a complete wardrobe system or carry its later injury, weather, dirt and exhaustion back to her healthy arrival at nineteen. Later generated faces do not replace this reference. [Appearance and wardrobe](original-visuals.md#tessa-rusk) distinguishes source facts from working choices for build, hair and clothing.

The active [wardrobe redesign](../visual-novel/art/character-keys/tessa/wardrobe-redesign.md) uses layered sacred garments, shaped yokes, woven botanical borders and substantial mineral-colored textiles. The user responded positively to the long early working ensemble and explicitly retained trousers, especially for the trenches. Formal wear changes the undergarment, coat, fastening and footwear, not just an outer mantle. The formal and late campaign keys remain working studies; winter, intermediate fieldwear and postwar personal clothing still need development. The old modern-looking jacket, formal trouser suit and cardigan are superseded costume references.

Drawings, notebook, dead/restored phone, traveling coat, temple mantle and badge, learned sword use, white sanctuary and the final hand brace form a visual vocabulary. The coat must evolve with circumstance rather than remaining an unchanged base costume under every new layer.

The [age and wardrobe progression](../visual-novel/art/character-keys/age-and-wardrobe.md#tessa) keeps her recognizable from nineteen to about twenty-three without freezing her condition at arrival. The postwar design must carry visible aging, exhaustion, grief and protection of the damaged right hand; the user rejected both the comfortable smiling study and a merely pouty correction. New clothing does not erase those consequences, and the direction does not authorize invented major scars or premature injury.
