# Fantasy setting and institutions

Status: current original-route facts plus clearly identified earlier altered-draft material. This is a gazetteer of the work we have, not a newly invented atlas. The planet's name, continental map, full northern polity name, distances and complete social history remain open.

The original VN now has [working location designs](original-locations.md) covering the settings used in Rebuild 11: construction, everyday life, room layouts, action geography and changes over time. New physical details are identified as adaptation choices; they do not establish a full atlas or an art style.

## Current original setting

**Rovel.** The human royal/temple center where Tessa is summoned. Its temple, palace, infirmaries, archives, council rooms and convoy arrangements place religious and military authority in her daily life. Senn directs the summoning program; Marshal Orra represents military command. Their interests overlap but their powers are not interchangeable. The current draft's concession to Elin is limited; an order does not make every archive or official cooperative.

**Bellweir.** A human river fortress and lived-in market community. Its locks matter to northern navigation; its bakery, fountain, apprentices and families make its destruction personal. The rotating glass heron is a persistent landmark. Ward-supported foundations beneath houses connect military sabotage to civilian disaster. The eastern causeway is an escape route across the river into open country. The bakery belongs to Hest and Orren. A later rebuilt fountain still has a missing wing in the original ending; both wings survive in the older altered ending.

**The river corridor.** Three fortresses and their locks constrain northern transport. Barges, grain, timber, ferries, quarries, road conditions and displaced families give the conflict material interests beyond royal hostility. Captured crews and forced labor are part of the current war. Access depends on transport, personnel, local command, private interests and fear, not just the existence of the right letter.

**Gray Scar.** Frontier approaches converge on a timber ferryhouse. An iron gate protects the landing under a rock overhang; two boats create distinct evacuation choices. Predatory crag cats are independent of both armies. Their attacks trap vulnerable human and northern parties, forcing bounded cooperation. The geography must support the rescue without returning to a detailed obstacle inventory that overwhelms the people.

**The river camps and quarry.** Prisoners, laborers, guards, healers, boatmen and camp authorities have different incentives. Lucan's rank is not unrestricted command. In the current draft he uses searches, witnesses, transport and concessions; the work has costs, and Tessa can challenge compromises that support the forced-labor system. A register is a lead, not a guarantee of a prisoner's location or release.

**Harrow Ford.** A covered bridge, tollhouse clock, cargo shed, stone approaches and a dangerous fall into water. A one-day exchange promises return to families. Valcair and Vask use tampered return wagons and southern-marked equipment to destroy the crossing and misdirect blame. The current mechanism is not the earlier under-bridge relay system. The bridge is where Iven dies in the original route and a major site whose meaning must change in the altered route.

**The occupied mill town.** A renewed barrier prevents evacuation. Tessa's failed direct attempts lead to an attack on its wardkeeper in a tower; Olan participates. It occurs three months after Harrow. The event tests her ability to act violently for others while Iven's absence remains acute.

**The northern citadel.** A royal stronghold with infirmary, stores, interior routes, galleries, towers, western passage, narrow stair bend and throne hall. Current Lucan protects witnesses and wounded there while Tessa leads the decisive assault. The hall's bronze doors and its magical obstruction are separate. The inner release and Valcair's personal armor spell are separate. Mara dies holding the stair; Tessa loses function in her drawing hand inside the hall.

## Earlier altered-draft places

**Mallow Reach.** Neri's coastal home, with tidal inlet, jetty, boatyard and the Vale sailmaking household. Eda works sailcloth; Jory works fittings; Lark is Neri's childhood friend and craft collaborator. Model-boat races grow into a two-seat winged skiff. This material grounds Neri in a real childhood and gives ordinary companions lives beyond the war.

**The west garden.** A quiet palace space where Tessa first encounters Pell and Doro at conversational scale. Its useful function is respite and voluntary company, not another appointment imposed on her.

**The bridge lodge.** Earlier draft accommodation becomes linked to a larger infirmary through a cupboard doorway. Preserve the changed experience of distance and care; re-evaluate the overly easy administrative setup.

**Northhaven and the northern strait.** Earlier draft sites for obstructed grain trade, broken watercourses, repaired wells and a naval blockade bypassed by Serein's airborne shipping. The exact map and scale are unpinned.

**The citadel garden hall, terrace and river meadow.** Earlier draft peace gathering links courts, kitchens, meadows and distant villages. A ramp transforms the stair associated with Mara's original death into a place of access and reunion. The gathering's dramatic function requires revision: the previous draft settled the war before its purported climax.

## People and institutions must continue to have interests

Healers distinguish curse removal from wound care. Officers have obligations, limits and loyalties. Workers and prisoners do not become grateful scenery. A king can receive reports without obtaining perfect information. A governor's permission does not dissolve guards' fear. Neri's altered interventions may remove practical dangers while leaving people to decide what relationships and institutions they want.
