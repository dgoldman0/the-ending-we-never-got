# Magic and practical limits

## Tessa's current original-route capabilities

**Saint purification.** Ordinary healers can mend living tissue while a curse continues killing it. Tessa can remove that persistent magical corruption. The first treatment demonstrates the distinction visually; neither professional expertise nor the reason a Saint was requested should vanish to manufacture surprise.

**Wound healing.** Tessa can help surviving tissue join and close minor wounds. Missing fingers or substantial lost tissue do not simply regrow. Serat's injury receives actual aid but still requires surgical care. Her eventual hand injury remains consequential. Do not make the final disability depend on a healing option being silently forgotten.

**Sanctuary.** Protective light can cover people, deflect attacks and support falling material. Spread, concentrated load and competing demands affect what she can sustain. A broken barrier does not itself produce a broken bone: impacts, footing, masonry and weapons need physical causes.

**Maintaining versus casting.** Training establishes that a maintained effect can leave a hand free. Tessa uses both hands to try to save Iven while sustaining a shelter. Another demanding effect can compete with the first; the final fight uses concentration and a deliberate change of spell rather than inventing a one-handed restriction.

**Selective interaction.** Her shelter does not automatically erase friendly wards. She can deliberately purify an opposing magical effect. Holding a roof and stripping armor are different uses of her power. Establish that distinction in action; do not repeat a modes-of-magic lesson during every crisis.

**Active opposition.** A wardkeeper can renew an effect faster than she clears it, withdraw behind cover, and attack. Valcair renews his armor protection and prevents concentration with aggressive attacks. The final encounter depends on his continuing choices, Tessa's training and her tactical commitment.

## World magic in the current draft

Healers, soldiers, wardkeepers and summoners have distinct practices. Wards protect bodies and structures; corruption can compromise protective foundations. Summoning is an institutional ritual with incomplete surviving records. It succeeds in bringing Tessa while offering no verified reverse procedure. Exact cosmology and a complete taxonomy are not yet established.

Keep the spectacular and emotional experience legible. Earlier cuff, silver hoop, black thread and magical wiring devices were removed. Reintroducing one requires a new authorized design decision, not a synonym substitution.

## Neri and the familiar company — altered development

Her High Human body provides an exceptional local physical/magical baseline. Much of the extraordinary breadth comes through the long-developed bond with her companions. The exact quantitative division between body, shared perception, familiar ability and direct magic remains open.

Earlier scenes demonstrate doorway links, remote observation through the bond, scale changes, precision water manipulation, lifting enormous volumes, structural support, airborne transport, memory projection and containment of dangerous charges. These are recovered draft implementations rather than a finalized universal spell list.

Pell's doors can connect separated locations. The earlier return-home scene traces the surviving summoning connection with identifying information from Tessa, rather than assuming a place name uniquely locates any Earth. Whether and how that connection is technically preserved needs reconciliation with the current original ritual.

Neri must remain overwhelmingly capable. Do not invent a last-minute equal enemy, resource shortage or inability purely to reproduce Tessa's original sacrifice. Suspense can concern choices, misunderstandings, changed plans and the discovery of how safety was achieved.

A familiar's independence, communication and decisions must be visible. Shared awareness is not permission for every character to know everything, nor a substitute for informing someone affected by a plan.
