# Neri's former reality, Astravus and reincarnation

## Protected foundation

Neri began as one ordinary human in roughly the present-day/near-future era, when Tessa's story existed as entertainment. Humanity later developed agelessness. Neri lived on for millions of years and accumulated relationships and experience through those ages. Iven and Mara's former human lives ended young, before that development.

Neri eventually became an Astravus through a long relationship with a vast nonhuman familiar company. The development concept retains constituent identities and preferences rather than erasing companions into obedient extensions. Neri's own ancient identity is nonbinary; the current local presentation uses she/her. Do not conflate appearance, body and the entire history of her identity.

Her reincarnation produces a young High Human body in the fantasy world. She is born there and grows up with local parents. The public sees unusual capabilities but does not know the classification or her former scale of existence.

## Recovered earlier-draft elaboration

The integrated draft depicts an expansion from shared senses to shared memories, ideas and action across bodies. Its memory sequence reveals a vast branching living body with waterways, forests and inhabited interiors moving through space. 'Home' and 'body' overlap without abolishing individuals' capacity to disagree or travel separately.

A failed crossing is offered as the immediate precursor to rebirth. The exact cause, metaphysics, survival of each companion and relationship between the two realities were not completed. Treat that explanation as draft scaffolding, not a solved cosmology.

That draft uses a little over six million years and a little over 300,000 companions. Preserve the figures as recoverable choices, not fixed numerical requirements. The essential scale is immense personal longevity and a genuinely large ensemble.

## Luna

A terraformed Luna with skyships, coasts, harbors and future-engineered life provides an occasional memory of Neri's long life. Serein's species is associated with those engineered skies in the earlier draft. Do not import technical lunar-terraforming calculations or make Luna the main plot.

The misunderstanding works because Neri refers naturally to places she lived, assuming the other fans also lived into that civilization. Iven and Mara assume she is a young contemporary and may interpret her account as another game, an eccentric story or a joke. Their clearer source memories do not imply a comparably long life.

The final realization includes the fact that ordinary people eventually received the future they died before seeing. It should have emotional weight alongside the absurd numerical surprise.

## Reveal constraints

Memory imagery is private, late and consent-sensitive. Only Tessa, Iven and Mara learn Neri's complete history. Eda, Jory, Lark, Lucan and Valcair do not automatically receive it because they witness spectacular abilities.

Neri's love for her new parents is genuine. An ancient prior life does not make their care a meaningless performance. Likewise, local Iven and Mara's families remain real despite their additional memories.

The ontological relation among the fans' reality, Tessa's Earth, the visual novel and the fantasy world remains open. Avoid claiming that the people are simulations, that Neri authored their world, or that a deterministic plot forces every event to recur.
