# Familiar company and working roster

## Protected ensemble requirement

Neri's power is a one-person-army-scale summoner concept, drawing especially on She Professed Herself Pupil of the Wise Man. Millions of years imply an immense, diverse history of companionship. A handful of named regulars helps the reader form attachments but does not limit the company to one or two overpowered pets.

Companions can include species not present on contemporary Earth, including organisms engineered during humanity's future. They retain preferences, relationships, expertise and the capacity to initiate or disagree. A useful test for a sequence is which companion notices something, selects a method, protects someone or changes a later event through its own choice.

The chosen relationship term is **bondkin**. In the earlier draft, familiars call Neri their bondkin. It is not a substitute word for master or owner. Do not import a cast from another project with the terminology.

## Wider company already represented

Silver moth formations; additional otters branching through waterways; broad-winged and feathered carriers; stone-backed companies; antlered creatures manipulating light and shelter; ribbon-winged aerial groups; small climbers carrying instruments; a small large-eared fox-like companion; a long-necked flower-bringer. These groups are recovery notes, not a fixed species taxonomy.

The older draft shows 418 deployed around Bellweir and more than 300,000 in the full company. Those counts should not become a ceiling or an obligatory roster. Its wide formations sometimes function too much like equipment. Future work must preserve their independent life.

## Designing cuteness and spectacle

Give creatures recognizable attention, movement, touch, preferences and social behavior. Cuteness should not come only from small size or an announcement that something is adorable. Scale reveals need a clear baseline, a perceptible change and differentiated human reactions. The mundane behavior can continue at the larger scale, but safety and physical geography remain legible.

Neri's enthusiasm is sincere. Companions can share affection for Tessa without every one mirroring Neri's personality. The ensemble should support different kinds of humor, competence and companionship.

## Open design work

Final roster size; exact ages and origins of each familiar; naming of wider formations; visual sheets; rules governing embodied access after reincarnation; distinctions between individually initiated action and shared coordination; how much of the company can be perceived before the late disclosure.
