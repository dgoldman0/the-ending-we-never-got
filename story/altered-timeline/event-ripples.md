# Original-to-altered consequence map

This is a planning matrix, not a claim that the new altered screenplay has been written. The original column follows Rebuild 11; recovered older implementations are explicitly labeled.

| Original cause/event | Protected altered aim / recovered idea | Consequences to carry forward |
|---|---|---|
| Tessa arrives without consent and is confined | Reincarnated Iven/Mara prepare immediate care and boundaries; Neri recognizes the world around the summoning | Initial fear remains, but the formation of trust and the specific coercion history can differ. Do not transfer Mara's original guilt unchanged. |
| Senn exploits an unverified promise of return | Earlier exposure and practical help; older draft restores contact and later passage home | Tessa can participate voluntarily. Jo becomes an active relationship; later schedules and motivations must remember access home. |
| Tessa's healing is appropriated institutionally | Support her learning and agency without removing the reality of need | More competence need not mean more imposed service. The helpers' availability does not make her choices irrelevant. |
| Bellweir sabotage destroys homes and separates Orren | Prevent the sabotage and any adaptive secondary operation | No refugee/disappearance chain solely caused by that destruction. Reconsider prisoner searches, Hest's losses and first prince contact. |
| Gray Scar forces bounded cooperation | Keep an independently credible route to mutual respect if this event survives the new causal chain | Neri may contain or relocate the threat. The prince still needs a voluntary revealing choice, not merely a chance to watch her rescue everyone. |
| Camps deny information and release | Stronger evidence/transport can change bargaining while officials and prisoners retain interests | Do not preserve identical gate scenes after their prerequisites vanish. Companions help without erasing Lucan's agency. |
| Lucan makes costly choices for access | Preserve his competence and values under changed conditions | He may spend his efforts differently when Neri removes scarcity or danger. Do not reset him to a decorative helpful prince. |
| A limited exchange becomes viable | Safe returns and new relationships are allowed to accelerate events | Iven/Mara's remembered dates become less reliable because of actual changes, not arbitrary schedule tricks. |
| Valcair tries to sabotage Harrow | Neri/company remain ahead of the attempt and prevent casualties | Iven lives; witnesses survive; the intended propaganda loses its victims. Valcair and Vask must respond to that failure. |
| Tessa suspects Lucan after absence, recall and Iven's death | Judge the new prince encounter from altered evidence and conduct | No automatic original rupture, but no automatic absolution of every decision. Separate safe outcome, responsibility and personal trust. |
| Iven dies and later care is marked by absence | He reaches the future he planned beyond Harrow | Veranda, brother, patients and ordinary time supply more than 'Iven survived.' His prior fear of his own death can still matter. |
| Tessa must win through lethal war | Her heroism develops in a safer, expanded world | Keep initiative and real preferences. Do not require a replacement wound or death for character growth. |
| Lucan's investigation is resisted | Existing fan lore and company observation can change the information problem | Preserve what was witnessed versus inferred. Do not solve it with a perfect recovered murder order. |
| Human-initiated ancient history is unknown to the main cast | Reincarnated fans may already know the explicit VN lore | They can act on it; local people still need reasons/evidence to accept claims. Neri need not stage a redundant 'first discovery.' |
| War threatens grain, movement and civilian work | Overwhelming logistics and particular relationships make coercive strategies ineffective | Enemies update their assumptions. Changed civilian incentives carry into the king's choices. |
| Valcair dies in Tessa's final duel | His interest in war collapses through failed schemes and a life he comes to want | Preserve voluntary peace and responsibility. The final event must complete something unresolved without conventional conquest. |
| Mara dies on the stair | She survives into family commitments and shared future | Reuse the place's meaning through access and leisure, not another fake death scare. |
| Tessa's hand is permanently damaged | She retains her hand and drawing future | Final writing/sketching can mirror the original naturally; avoid an explanatory speech about the missing injury. |
| Neri's truth is unknown | Late private disclosure to Tessa/Iven/Mara only | Public spectacles do not make the biography public. Full revelation can connect earlier signs while leaving local attachments intact. |
| Original home search remains unresolved | Earlier draft allows actual visits and chosen return to fantasy work | The final holiday can include both families; original Earthlessness must not leak into this ending. |

## Regression questions for any mirrored event

What caused it in the original? Does that cause still exist? What did Neri, Iven, Mara, Lucan and the company each contribute? Who knows what afterward? Which relationships, resources, injuries, absences and duties have changed? Which later scene now needs replacement rather than a line edit?
