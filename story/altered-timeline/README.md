# Altered timeline — recovered plan and current direction

This directory preserves the outer story that was missing from the repository. It is **development material**, not a newly revised screenplay. The last integrated version was The Ending We Never Got, Editorial Revision 3; original-route work has since advanced through Rebuild 11.

Current status (16 September 2026): incursion-timeline development is deferred while work focuses on an [original-timeline Ren'Py VN](../../visual-novel/README.md). The questions about Neri and the reincarnated Iven/Mara remain available for a later return.

Read [premise](../../project/premise.md), [knowledge boundaries](../knowledge-and-reveals.md), [event ripples](event-ripples.md), and the [recovered sequence register](recovered-sequences.md).

## Protected course

Neri is born in this world years before Tessa arrives. Her extraordinary former life and companions gradually become available as she grows, while recognition of this specific setting clicks around the summoning. She has parents, a childhood and friendships here; they are not a disguise.

Reincarnated Iven and Mara already remember the VN and have been trying to improve the approaching events. They hold the jobs, relationships and obligations of their local lives. When Tessa is summoned, they can use professional authority and preparations without pretending to have unlimited command.

Neri's attachment to Tessa is intense and exuberant. Her company is enormous and capable of simultaneous interventions. A few recurring companions introduce the relationship; later combinations and formations reveal a one-person-army scale. Neither the number of foreground pets nor the local institutions' understanding defines her actual limit.

Every major avoided tragedy changes subsequent causes. Saving Bellweir preserves its people, homes and infrastructure. Preventing Harrow preserves Iven, makes an attempted betrayal materially different and leaves witnesses alive. Preventing the final sacrificial battle permits Mara and Tessa a shared future. The purpose is not to keep the original itinerary intact while replacing each death with a rescue cameo.

By the end, Valcair's schemes are overwhelmed to bewilderment, their usefulness disappears and his interests change through the world and relationships around him. Peace becomes voluntary. Neri does not need to defeat him in a conventional final duel. Tessa retains meaningful heroism, choice and potentially a successful relationship with Lucan.

## Earlier draft's positive-life material worth retaining

Tessa regains contact with her mother and can make actual visits; Neri's own parents meet her; Iven commits to a veranda beyond the date he expected to die; Mara attends a niece's birthday; Lark and Neri finish a winged skiff; a patient asks for comfort because she likes it, not because treatment requires it; the formerly fatal stair becomes a usable ramp and gathering place; Tessa writes freely with her uninjured right hand at the four-year mirror ending.

These are specific expressions of a changed future. They should not be crowded out by logistics, repeated astonishment or another long briefing about how everything was arranged.

## Work still required

The original draft now has a different first-cooperation mechanism, more capable Lucan, resisted investigations, different Harrow sabotage and much stronger trust consequences. The altered sequence has not been rebuilt to absorb them. The older script uses Cael, British diction, easy paperwork and a prematurely resolved war. Those are preservation cautions, not features to restore.

The audience can see some familiar interventions before the full revelation. The late disclosure should connect previously meaningful clues and activity rather than announce that Neri secretly did everything while the other cast waited.

The finer distribution of comedy, cuteness, flirtation and shock reactions remains open. Avoid allocating all intelligence to Neri and all bafflement to everyone else.
