# Recovered altered-story sequence register

Source: the complete altered portion of The_Ending_We_Never_Got_Editorial_Revision_3.fountain, original file lines 617–4548, read during this preservation pass. This register retains its event ideas, personal-life material and worldbuilding while identifying major conflicts with later decisions. Dialogue has not been adopted wholesale. 'Prince' below means the character now named Lucan; the source called him Cael.

## A. Neri's local life and recognition

**Birth.** A storm rattles the Vale coastal house. Eda holds the baby while Jory fixes a window latch; a silver moth listens from the sill. Neri's birth follows the original story's fade to black, nineteen years before the summoning.

**Sailmaking at six.** Eda teaches Neri to sew. Pell helps with thread. Neri knows a knot she cannot place in memory and has difficulty executing it with small fingers; Eda wants to learn it. The scene makes local care and older knowledge coexist.

**Model boats at eleven.** Neri and Lark race boats. Neri's improvised spoon rudder fails; an otter returns it. Jory's practical advice and the retained broken spoon give the household a shared story. Do not turn a local craft mishap into a universal limit on her later power.

**The boatyard at nineteen.** Neri and Lark work on a winged skiff. A gazette reports the Saint summoning and names Tessa Rusk; a shopping-bag woodcut triggers memories of the bridge and the heroine. Neri finally recognizes the world, becomes intensely excited, packs and travels with Pell through a hidden doorway.

## B. The reincarnated pair and the first encounter

**Altered summoning.** Iven catches the milk carton; Mara arranges space and immediate care rather than ceremonial pressure. Tessa is still frightened and displaced. This mirror must be reconciled with the current original opening rather than copied literally.

**Care and privacy.** The earlier draft gives Tessa rest, food and a room key. The useful direction is professional care and autonomy. Its easy dismissals of institutional demands must be checked against real authority and resistance.

**Private notebook.** Iven and Mara record deviations and preparations. Years of contacts, staffing and cart work predate the summoning. A former-life glimpse shows repeated bridge saves in the VN; the pair knows that they could not obtain the desired result in the game. They now have actions available beyond menu choices.

**Neri enters the archive.** Linen from her sailmaking home helps Elin handle damaged pages. Tessa's dropped glass becomes a first precise familiar demonstration. Neri's rehearsed introduction collapses into delighted awkwardness. Mara asks why she has come and checks the family connection.

**Garden respite.** Pell speaks while being scratched; Doro arrives at dog scale and lets Tessa decide whether to approach. Neri nearly talks through the quiet, then gives her room. This is voluntary company and creature behavior, not a service appointment.

**Recognition between fans.** A tune identifies a shared source memory. Iven and Mara reveal their former Earth lives to Neri without grasping her age. They show her the notebook. Lace begins observing a person and deliveries connected with the anticipated salt sabotage. Exact timing of this partial disclosure remains to be confirmed against the protected late full reveal.

## C. Home and chosen participation

**Earlier exposure of Senn.** The old draft used a conveniently explicit authorization. Preserve early protection of Tessa's autonomy; replace that obsolete discovery mechanism with the current knowledge and institutional constraints.

**Tracing the crossing.** Elin's ritual fragments, Pell and the surviving connection combine with Tessa's identification of her street and photographs. Neri restores the phone's charge. A small opening restores a signal before a larger doorway reaches home. These are proposed mechanics, not powers available to original-route Tessa.

**Jo answers.** Tessa's mother has been searching for her. The first call and physical reunion interrupt ordinary kitchen life. The retained milk carton becomes a personal callback, not broad slapstick.

**Time at home.** Tessa wants days with her mother and worries that the fantasy world's need will pull her back. Neri promises continuing access and personal visits rather than making help contingent on service. The following week Tessa chooses a limited medical trip and retains plans at home. Jo asks whether she herself wants to go.

**Two households.** Neri writes to Eda; Eda sends clothing and skiff news. The coat comes from the Vale household. Jo, Eda and later the wider families can form relationships without receiving Neri's private cosmic biography.

## D. Bellweir is preserved

**Repeated ordinary encounter.** Ada's scraped elbow, the head joke, Hest's pastries and the glass heron recur. Neri recognizes the lines she loved; Mara moves a tray out of the way of her excitement. A repaired drum enters the community and returns at the gathering.

**Learning and fan delight.** Tessa learns to sustain a sanctuary while moving. Neri's premature celebration distracts her; Tessa asks her to wait until the attempt is finished. Her achievement matters to Neri despite being small relative to the company.

**Water contamination.** The old draft combines medical treatment, Lace's sampling, Brim and other otters following waterways, sealed contamination and attention to affected households. Mica protects a road so dangerous jars survive long enough to be examined. The current-world requirement is causally acquired evidence, not another perfect ledger waiting under a desk.

**The Luna misunderstanding.** On a balcony, Neri casually remembers skyships departing lunar harbors. Iven interprets this as another game or eccentric memory. The interruption must be credible, not a repeated device to prevent obvious clarification.

**A private preference corrects the source image.** Neri thinks Tessa likes the green seed pastry because of the remembered scene. Tessa had been politely accepting it. Pell likes it. This lets the real person exceed what an audience inferred from fiction.

**Prevented foundation sabotage.** The earlier draft catches Harven and lets Mica support the tower while masons repair the actual joint. Human expertise and familiar capability cooperate. The old arrest paperwork is not approved as an effortless solution.

**The flood contingency.** Vask turns to river engines after the original sabotage fails. Doro becomes immense and lifts the water clear; Brim's group isolates engines, Mica's companies open channels and antlered/moth groups remove corruption. Tessa shelters and reassures the square. Fish swimming in the suspended river give scale a concrete image.

**Known flaw.** Neri's failure to tell Tessa she was safe was identified as inconsistent with her protectiveness. Do not retain manufactured terror so the reveal can be spectacular. Competent enemies also need to update their understanding after this display.

**Afterward.** The fountain and family are intact. A count of 418 companions surprises the pair but is a draft number. Their roles and independent choices matter more than tallying them. Neri uses the bondkin term. Tessa later tells Hest her actual pastry preference.

## E. Contact, prisoners and a life beyond duty

**The prince investigates survivors.** In the old draft, the king sends him after reports of a table-sized dragon and a contained engine. Scouts describe their treatment. Current Lucan's independent initiative and bounded authority must replace the easy official-meeting progression.

**Medical access and spatial care.** Prisoner treatment, a cramped bridge lodge and a cupboard opened onto a distant quiet ward demonstrate what changed distance could mean to care. Keep patient agency, security concerns and institutional interests. A door alone does not grant consent to use another hospital.

**Travel and boot linings.** Tessa learns riding; the prince helps with painful boots; they turn that observation toward prisoners' footwear. Cobb manages camp heat. These are relationship and craft seeds, but the old version overused helpful work as the entire basis of romance.

**Home conversation.** Jo notices that Tessa's account keeps returning to the prince and asks whether they ever spend time together outside illness. Neri repairs a wheelbarrow that then follows Jo. The concrete home contact distinguishes an actual ongoing relationship from a vague claim that Tessa remains connected to home.

**Mara's family.** Ness and Mara sew Pet's dress; Mara commits to attending the birthday. The calendar and covered duty become a future that original Mara lacked.

**Iven's veranda.** Dann works on a recovery-space design. Iven orders timber for a date beyond his expected death. The decision carries hope and uncertainty before Harrow rather than adding an obituary-style hobby afterward.

## F. Harrow becomes survival

**An accelerated exchange.** With Bellweir still standing, returns occur earlier than the remembered original date. Foreknowledge must respond to changed causes and timing. Iven still recognizes the bridge and bell as the location of his expected death.

**Containment and different routes.** The old draft detects three relay charges, contains them and moves people through destination doorways across a meadow instead of exposing them to the bridge. The current original route uses tampered cargo, so this mechanism must be redesigned; its desired result is protected survival, not a compulsory three-relay puzzle.

**The prince stays.** He learns the attempt and rejects his recall, choosing to remain with the families. Tessa's changed grounds for judgment must be written from what she sees and knows in this version. Do not copy the original rupture unchanged if Neri has materially changed its causes.

**The bell without a death.** Tessa fastens Renn's boot while Iven attends another patient. The fourth bell passes with the bridge empty and safe. Later he can name the work he will be present to complete. Relief can be overwhelming without restoring danger for a second climax.

**Father and son.** The old prince confronts Valcair with passenger lists and attempted sabotage. Current Lucan's stronger character should govern the new encounter. The parent's love must not absolve his intent or instantaneously change him.

## G. Pleasure, work and the collapse of military leverage

**Visit to Mallow Reach.** Tessa meets Eda and Jory, sees Neri's height marks and birthday cloth birds, and learns the history behind her coat. Lark has finished the skiff; Jory still displays the spoon rudder.

**Flight.** Tessa chooses how high to go. Serein and her formation travel beside the skiff, light passing through their bodies. Neri enjoys Tessa's discovery; Lark teases them afterward. This should remain actual leisure, not a hidden training requirement.

**Blockade and villages.** The old king tries a northern strait blockade while villages need food and repaired watercourses. Serein's company carries laden merchant craft above the fleet; ships still contain crews and washing. A pilot has to participate in an event his institution did not expect.

**Trade and repair.** Brim and Mica restore wells; timber and flour flows make civilian interests visible. The old harbor negotiation was too frictionless and the navy insufficiently adaptive. Preserve consequences of overwhelming power, not paper-thin enemies or universal instant cooperation.

**The king's visit.** Neri's absorption in Tessa contrasts with formal arrival. At Hest's bakery the attempted Harrow betrayal remains an issue. Repairs, compensation and withdrawal are proposed. This cannot be treated as a magical absolution or a completed development merely because a policy is signed.

**Brim's boat.** During a village visit Valcair becomes involved in a small model-boat problem, sees his son's life with Tessa and engages outside command. The seed is personal participation. The old transition from this to canceling the entire war is too compressed.

## H. Futures and disclosures

**Ordinary futures fulfilled.** Iven's veranda is built and has room for patients' preferences and visitors. Mara attends Pet's birthday. Tessa and the prince arrange time without patient lists. Neri agrees to leave them privacy, though her delight is hard to contain.

**Gathering preparations.** Hest closes the bakery so the apprentices can attend too. Invitations and rooms involve actual people from earlier events. Neri's gates make attendance possible without demanding weeks of travel.

**The stair triggers questions.** Mara knows details of the stair where her original counterpart died. Tessa notices the shared glances and insists on an explanation. The location gives the reveal a personal trigger.

**Source story revealed.** In private, Iven and Mara explain former Earth lives, the VN and the local lives they genuinely lived. Tessa asks about Iven's death, Mara's stair, the prince and home. She can refuse to view more and demand that future knowledge be shared before others decide for her.

**Age misunderstanding resolved.** Ordinary computer memories lead back to Luna. The earlier draft gives Iven and Mara specific near-future death years and Neri six million years; those exact numbers remain provisional. The fact that ordinary people later achieved agelessness affects the pair emotionally.

**Astravus memories.** Lunar skies, engineered species, early fandom, gardens, shared senses and an enormous living body connect her beginnings to the present. The High Human body and extent of the company are disclosed only to the three. Constituent identities survive shared experience.

**Retrospective coordination.** Earlier moths, waterways, foundations, roads, relays and rescued people are connected through the bond. This should explain already meaningful actions, not retroactively award every other character's work to Neri.

## I. Gathering and mirrored ending

**The formerly fatal stair changes purpose.** A ramp and landing carry Mara, her family and guests. Tessa can take her hand there. The place now allows people to meet and look at the river.

**Ensemble scale.** Pell's larger form opens approaches. Stone-backed groups lay paths; antlered companions support canopies; moths light routes; ribbon-winged formations transport visitors. Basil and his twelve spirits learn to work alongside the wider company. Cobb warms ovens; creatures bring seats, instruments and flowers.

**Public peace.** The earlier draft seats thousands in linked meadows and lets the king accept responsibility for repairs and continued travel. Its count of 6,240 guests against 800 ordinary seats is a draft gag, not a world fact that must survive.

**Known climax problem.** That draft cancels the campaign well before the gathering. A new version needs the finale to complete an outstanding transformation, choice or undertaking, consistent with the protected non-defeat peace. Do not add a surprise equal-power battle.

**Affection.** The prince and Tessa choose time together and kiss in the older ending; Neri is moved by the happier life around them. Pell says the companions love Tessa too. Preserve meaningful relationships without prematurely fixing their final romantic categories.

**Four-year mirror.** Both heron wings remain. Tessa writes with her uninjured right hand. Iven and Mara have time away from work. Jo and Eda arrive through a doorway; Lark and the prince help with boats and food. A return to Neri's crooked childhood cloth bird links her local life to shared leisure. The cast leaves for an ordinary outing together.
