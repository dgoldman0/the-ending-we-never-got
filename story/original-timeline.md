# Original Tessa timeline — current story map

Authority: [Rebuild 11 screenplay](../screenplay/original-timeline/source.fountain). This index preserves the course of the original route without reproducing every scene. It is not the altered timeline and contains no reincarnator foreknowledge.

## Arrival and attachment

1. Tessa's grocery errand is interrupted by involuntary summoning. The milk carton splits; her mother's ordinary message is still on her phone. The apparent passage back collapses. Senn promises an eventual return without a verified method.
2. Mara confines her under orders. Attempts to call home fail and the battery dies. Tessa draws her mother's kitchen on the reverse of the temple's welcome letter.
3. Tessa removes a curse from Olan's hand after ordinary treatment fails to stop the corruption. Missing fingers remain. The institutions turn the success into public expectation and deployment pressure.
4. Iven offers limited professional protection and company. Mara trains Tessa in sanctuary, selective purification and physical defense. Shared meals, checkers and the room key build relationships through actions.
5. Bellweir becomes a real community: the fountain, bakery, Hest, Orren, the children and apprentices. Tessa's drawings, teasing and a boat outing give those people life before the losses.

## War and autonomy

6. Valcair rejects a limited river agreement and chooses to attack the ward-supported fortress. Bellweir's foundations fail. Tessa saves the people within reach while Orren and the apprentices are cut off and subsequently taken.
7. The displaced family waits for news. Tessa and others search. Elin notices inconsistencies in the return material, obtains the original at personal risk and questions a reluctant participant. There is no convenient complete confession attached to the book.
8. Senn is confronted. He cannot establish a reliable return method. Tessa secures greater control over her work and an explicit end to confinement. Elin loses archive access; the concession is limited copying/help rather than unrestricted resources. Mara's earlier coercion remains a wound in their relationship.

## Lucan and limited cooperation

9. In the second summer, crag cats trap human and northern parties at Gray Scar. Each has vulnerable people. Distrust delays cooperation until complementary capabilities and concrete decisions permit escape.
10. Lucan returns for the final human litter after his own families are safe. Serat is wounded. Tessa helps but cannot replace the missing flesh; the group takes him to a human surgeon.
11. Mara keeps the promise of Lucan's departure despite the picket's attempt to detain him. Sanctions follow. Lucan loses command; this does not conveniently grant him the camp authority he needs.
12. Lucan undertakes searches and transport bargains, encounters refusal, helps obtain restricted treatment and release, and pays costs including his boat. Tessa can oppose a method that benefits forced labor while still valuing what he accomplishes. Their affection grows through particular interactions.

## Harrow and rupture

13. A limited exchange finally promises the return of Orren and the apprentices. Tessa relies on Lucan's specific promise to stay beside the cart before reassuring Hest about bringing the children.
14. Valcair and Vask deliberately sabotage returned cargo and misdirect blame through southern equipment. Lucan receives a recall; the audience sees him remain at the ford, but Tessa experiences his absence from her group.
15. The bridge fails. Iven frees Renn and nearly reaches safety. Tessa grasps him with both hands while maintaining protection, but failing structure tears him from her. Her further rescue attempt fails.
16. Tessa suspects Lucan, rejects his touch and sends him away. Mara bars personal access; information must come through her. Immediate grief and the later recruitment response have separate consequences. Hest's care for a northern searcher is a specific choice, not general reconciliation.

## A costly successful campaign

17. Three months later, a wardkeeper prevents evacuation despite Tessa's direct attempts. She and Olan enter his tower; she kills him to protect Olan. The aftermath centers on wanting Iven and still feeling the dead man's grip, rather than narrating the fight again.
18. The war continues across seasons. Tessa becomes more effective but cannot be everywhere. Lucan's investigation alerts opposition; Marren is moved and access obstructed. Lucan must extract and protect the clerk and his wife, suffering injury and political consequences.
19. Marren admits his own signature and limited observations. He did not watch the charges being installed and cannot establish everything Lucan knew. Tessa's interrogation and eventual reading of a letter do not automatically restore intimacy.
20. Lucan confronts his father and refuses demands that would surrender the witness. Some captains reject him; others help defend the infirmary. He sends information that warns of changed watches rather than a magically perfect plan. Olan checks the exterior approach independently.
21. Tessa undertakes the decisive assault while Lucan fights on his own front. Mara holds the stair. Tessa's opportunity closes behind her; she confronts Valcair and changes tactics against his actively renewed defenses.
22. Valcair wounds her right hand. She kills him, opens the hall and enables the next military movement. Mara has died before their promised reunion. Her drawing-hand injury remains lasting.
23. A limited cessation becomes possible; Marren's protection and Harrow's unresolved responsibility remain relevant. Lucan's political role does not entitle him to comfort Tessa personally.
24. Four years after summoning, Bellweir is rebuilding. Tessa refuses temple accommodation, remains among people she loves and continues seeking a route to Earth with Elin. A possible book lead remains uncertain. She has a worthwhile future alongside grief and disability.

## Do not compress away these distinctions

Success versus restoration; evidence versus forgiveness; professional authority versus unlimited permission; purification versus tissue regrowth; private character knowledge versus audience knowledge; a quiet future versus a cheerful account of everything she endured.
