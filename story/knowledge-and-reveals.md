## Protected presentation order

Begin with Tessa's original tragedy as the apparent immediate story. Return to Neri's birth and local growth. Let the reader discover Iven and Mara's coordinated attempts relatively early in the altered story; their knowledge is real and specific. Reveal Neri's full history very late, and only to Tessa, Iven and Mara.

The earlier draft has two private discussions within the late sequence: first Earth lives and Tessa's source story, then the age/Luna misunderstanding and Astravus history. That structure is useful but not a mandate to deliver a long explanatory block without interruption or consequences.

## Fair misunderstanding

Shared references to an old VN do not establish equal elapsed lifetimes. Neri can take the others' fresher recall as evidence that they preserved copies; they can take her Luna stories as fiction or eccentricity. However, misunderstanding must not survive an unmistakable correction merely because the plot wants a reveal later.

A remembered tune or menu can recognize another fan without explaining the entire cosmology. Public observation of portals, dragons and skyships does not confer private biographical knowledge.

## Tessa's agency during disclosure

She is entitled to ask why information was withheld, what her friends thought would happen to them, and whether she returned home in the original story. She can refuse to view an image of her suffering. Gratitude, anger and continuing affection can coexist. The changed timeline should not give her a second life controlled by other people's private interpretation of her needs.
