## Working applications

Distinguish what the story needs to establish from what each person wants to do with the other person now. The first belongs in the planning notes. The second shapes the speech. A reply may answer, evade, mishear, accuse or enthusiastically agree, but its form needs a local reason.

A fixed script can still suggest people discovering a conversation. Develop thoughts through changing emphasis and intention; do not merely join clipped statements with conjunctions. Explore an interaction before compressing it into a tidy delivery of required information.

Give action a continuing physical life. Work, location, fatigue, objects and other people affect attention. Do not prescribe a glance, handoff, pause or explanatory reaction between every two lines. A detail earns space when it changes what happens or what someone understands.

Keep direct emotional admissions available. 'I want Iven' can be more specific and exposed than another symbolic prop. Subtext is not a ban on honest speech, and asymmetry is not a command that everybody dodge every question.

## External evaluator's suggestions and limits

The evaluator identified utilitarian mechanics speech at Gray Scar, stacked moral conclusions with Senn, and a deposition-like exchange with Marren. Those diagnoses informed Rebuild 11's interaction-level pass.

Specific suggested replacements were not adopted as canon. 'Melting shields' would invent damage absent from the scene. Asking whether Lucan watched Marren wire a floor would invent observations and participation. A polished insult may be just as scripted as the moral conclusion it replaces. Preserve knowledge, magic, props and plot outcomes while applying the intended pressure.

## Known project failure cycle

Early drafts used serial paratactic declarations. Later passes lengthened speech and overexplained causal repairs; action then became microblocking. A subtractive pass reduced that bulk but could not by itself create responsive conversations. The present unit of drafting is the interaction plus its necessary action, with a full-scene reading window and downstream checks.
