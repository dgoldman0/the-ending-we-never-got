# All eight archetypes

This is the user's reference set, recovered from the prior development and review materials. The functions below are project applications, not claims of a new complete viewing or objective audience testing.

| Work | Function for this project | Import boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Heroine? Saint? No, I'm an All-Works Maid (And Proud of It)! | Intense personal enthusiasm shapes extravagant actions; emotional antics and affectionate excess inform Neri | Do not copy the maid identity, permanent obliviousness or distinctive cast. Her recognition should respond to evidence. |
| She Professed Herself Pupil of the Wise Man | A broad, coordinated familiar/summoner ensemble with one-person-army scale; combinations and simultaneous activity | Do not reduce it to one or two strong pets or merely cite a large number offscreen. |
| The New Gate | Shin's High Human baseline and experienced exceptional capability | Do not copy a skill list. Neri's local High Human identity stays private until late. |
| Kuma Kuma Kuma Bear | Power producing particular relationships, creature affection and continuing community consequences | Kindness and an easy rescue alone do not create a distinctive relationship. |
| By the Grace of the Gods | Companion-specific functions, shared work, personal enthusiasm and found-family warmth | Do not make useful work the only activity or turn the project into a business subplot. |
| I Shall Survive Using Potions! | Resourceful applications of extraordinary abilities and obstacles that remember established powers | Do not invent convenient new scarcity or forget an available ability simply to force a scene. |
| My Next Life as a Villainess: All Routes Lead to Doom! / Bakarina | Attempts to change remembered fiction cause further relationship changes; personality drives events | The original Iven/Mara are natives; altered versions have local lives plus memories. Do not equate narrative change with NPC control. |
| Fluffy Paradise | Creature attention, touch, preference, cuteness and social consequences | Gentle presentation must still carry moral weight. Cuteness does not absolve atrocities or require every wild threat to become a pet. |

## Tonal allocation

Neri's altered story carries the strongest OP comedy, gushing, shock reactions and endearing creatures. The original Tessa telling remains an emotionally compelling, painful heroic story. Familiarity with a reference is not a substitute for information or development on this page.

Additional comparisons already used: The Saint's Magic Power Is Omnipotent for the difference between uncertainty about Saint identity and uncertainty about a mechanism; Grimgar of Fantasy and Ash for losses changing survivors' behavior and relationships.

## Screenplay studies already consulted

The Princess Bride: changes in narrative frame and who controls an exchange. How to Train Your Dragon: creature behavior, distance, movement and physical relationship. The Mitchells vs. the Machines: visual escalation and different reactions to absurd events. Everything Everywhere All at Once: maintaining personal relationships through changes in scale and reality.

The original research notes describe selected passages, not cover-to-cover study of all four scripts. Their first inventory recovered only four anime titles; later work restored the eight above. That earlier incomplete list is superseded.
